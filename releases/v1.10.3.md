# Ocean 1.10.3

- **Soft rose and soft beige anemones:** Replaces the overly bright snow-white anemone shading from 1.10.2 with natural soft rose (*leicht rosa*) and warm sand-beige (*leicht beige*) tentacle colonies and gently shaded bulbous tips (`Knöllchen`), preserving the matte, non-bluish underwater surface look.
- **Right-anemone rock collider & fish routing fix:** Replaces the discontinuous anemone crown box cutout with an exact continuous ellipsoid collider matching the right cliff of `rock 2` (`x <= 3.75`) and keeps non-shelter fish (such as the blue Regal Tang) from getting trapped or clipping into the rock wall beside the right anemone.
- **Unchanged clownfish pairs & camera passes:** Both female/male clownfish pairs continue to cuddle right through the left and right anemone colonies, part tentacles along their full length, and make periodic foreground passes in front of the camera.

Phone battery savings have not been measured.
