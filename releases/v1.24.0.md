# Ocean 1.24.0 (Beta) — Natural waves

Ocean 1.24.0 (Beta) adds a third way to draw the water surface seen from below. Under **Light & Water**, **Surface waves** now offers **Natural** beside Classic and Lively:

- **Natural waves:** waves come from all directions, cross, pile up into peaks and fade again. Large waves gather the small ones on their backs, the hollows between them stay smooth, and the same happens at every size, from waves of several metres down to ripples of a few centimetres. Lively waves, by comparison, travel mostly one way and can show a quilted pattern when you look sideways.
- **Wave strength** now reaches from 10 % (a nearly still bay) to 300 % (a rough sea), for lively and natural waves. With natural waves a calm sea is also smoother and a rough one more peaked.
- **Wave tempo (natural)** is new: 10 to 400 %, default 100 %.
- **Wave count** also sets how much fine detail the natural waves show.

Nothing changes unless you choose the new style: the default stays Lively, and your saved settings are kept.

Natural waves follow the complete recipe of afl_ext's "Very fast procedural ocean" (MIT licence, credited in the app and in the notices since 1.15.0), of which the lively waves use a part. No new models, textures or other third-party content were added.

## Cost

Measured on a Galaxy S21 at 720p: in views of the reef, natural waves cost the same graphics time as the other styles. Looking steeply up, where the water surface fills the screen, they need about 40 % more graphics work than lively waves at the standard wave count, and about the same at the lowest wave count. No battery claim is made, and the Galaxy S24 was not tested.

## Known limits

- With natural waves selected, the light shafts still follow the pattern of the lively waves.
- Natural waves have no main direction of travel.
- The lock screen itself was not looked at. This is a beta.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.24.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.24.0).
