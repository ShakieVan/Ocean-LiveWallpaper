# Ocean 1.13.1 (Beta) — Optional spirit-level horizon alignment vs. display alignment

Ocean 1.13.1 (Beta) adds a dedicated **Spirit-level alignment** (*Wasserwaagen-Ausrichtung*) toggle under **Tilt & Gyroscope**:

- **Optional spirit-level alignment (`spiritLevel`, default on):**
  - **When enabled (default):** automatically aligns the ocean horizon like a spirit level (*Wasserwaage*) with Earth's gravity vector at calibration, after the 2-second post-unlock reset, and after 5 seconds of holding still.
  - **When disabled:** always aligns the horizon to the **display (`0 rad`)** — initial calibration, the 2-second post-unlock reset, and the 5-second quiet re-alignment all align the ocean horizon to the screen regardless of how the device is held, while active 3rd-axis rotation (`Rotate screen (3rd axis)`) continues to rotate 1:1 relative to the display baseline during motion.
  - Toggling the switch while running smoothly transitions ($\tau \approx 0.4\text{ s}$) between spirit-level horizon alignment and display alignment without jumps.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.13.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.13.1).
