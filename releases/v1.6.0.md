# Ocean 1.6.0 — Fine fluorescence and full coral redistribution

Version **1.6.0 / code 25** gives fish markings more presence, softens the
brightest coral areas and makes Refresh change the whole reef arrangement.

## Fine colour and fluorescence

- Nine new Acanthastrea colour variants replace the previous orange colonies.
  Each polyp stands individually on its own rock attachment. All nine variants
  appear on the left reef, in the middle valley and around the right tunnel
  in every prepared layout when this coral type is enabled. The solid bright-red
  reference variant is excluded.
- Acanthastrea fluorescence follows fine lips, spokes and mouth edges. Other
  mapped corals keep their emission on existing surface details instead of
  broad colour fields.
- Brain coral has darker tissue and quieter green areas. Only narrow borders
  of its green pigment fluoresce gently, including extra growth and distant
  detail levels.
- All eight fish types gain fine fluorescent stripes and outlines that follow
  their existing markings. The **Fluorescence** control still runs from
  0% off through 100% normal to an exaggerated 200%.

The new coral textures are generated interpretations of colour and structure
references, not recovered detail from a low-resolution photograph. The existing
broad turquoise-orange disc form remains a separate shape. Fish markings are
an artistic effect, not a biological fluorescence claim.

## Refresh changes the whole reef

The four prepared layouts now move **all 231 regular reef colonies**, including
large foreground corals, all five clams and optional extra growth. Every switch
between different layouts changes their actual positions. Your enabled species
and growth setting determine which colonies are shown.

Occupied shelter corals for small fish and the two anemonefish homes stay in
place. Each Acanthastrea variant travels with its own polyp and remains in its
reef region. Fish navigation follows the visible arrangement, keeping water
space open where another layout's coral is no longer present.

Use the **Refresh** icon beside **Set wallpaper**, or **Redistribute corals**
in **Corals & clams**. Species choices, camera settings and appearance controls
are retained. Camera fine controls remain under **Settings > Camera**.

## Verification and installation

Geometry and material checks, fish-navigation tests and native Android checks
passed. Emulator comparisons covered all four layouts across the left reef,
valley and tunnel, fluorescence at 0/100/200%, species and growth controls,
and wallpaper resume, visibility and surface recreation. Model portraits were
refreshed from the native renderer. These checks do not establish phone battery
life, sustained frame rate or long-term stability on a physical device.

Update through **Settings > Updates** (`Einstellungen > Updates`), or use the
[release downloads](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.6.0).
