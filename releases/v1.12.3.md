# Ocean 1.12.3 (Beta) — Coning-free Y-axis tilting at 45°, straight portrait initialization, and gentle 5-second quiet re-alignment

Ocean 1.12.3 (Beta) eliminates horizon drift when rocking the phone around its Y-axis in a comfortable 45° lap posture, keeps the initial horizon straight during normal portrait viewing, and adds a gentle 5-second quiet re-alignment that ignores minor tremors:

- **Coning-free incremental Z-rotation (`decoupledRollStep`):**
  - When holding the phone at ~45° in the lap, Earth's vertical azimuth axis has a $\cos 45^\circ \approx 70.7\%$ projection onto the display normal (Z-axis), so rocking back and forth around the Y-axis previously accumulated 3D wrist coning and unanchored `GAME_ROTATION_VECTOR` azimuth drift into a steady leftward horizon tilt.
  - `decoupledRollStep` computes the incremental Z-rotation between consecutive sensor samples using allocation-free column dot products (`yx - xy`, `xx + yy`, `dx`, `dy`) weighted by $w_z = \big(\Delta\theta_z^2 / (\Delta\theta_z^2 + 1.5\,(\Delta\theta_x^2 + \Delta\theta_y^2))\big)^2$, suppressing $>99.5\%$ of parasitic wrist/coning twist during X/Y tilts while keeping 1:1 rotation when turning the screen around the 3rd axis.
- **Straight portrait initialization (`PORTRAIT_DEADZONE_RAD`):**
  - Adds a smooth $\pm 8^\circ$ portrait deadzone (`PORTRAIT_DEADZONE_RAD = 0.14`, smoothstep to full spirit-level at $18^\circ$) so a casual $4^\circ\text{–}6^\circ$ hand tilt at $45^\circ$ in the lap initializes the horizon dead-straight (`0 rad`) instead of slightly tilted to the left.
- **Gentle 5-second quiet check (`isQuiet`, `QUIET_WINDOW_NANOS`, `JOLT_GRACE_NANOS`):**
  - Uses two 3-vector dot products (6 multiplies, zero trig/sqrt) to check whether the phone stayed within a $5^\circ$ cone over 5 seconds while ignoring brief tremors or jolts under $250\text{ ms}$, then smoothly decays any residual X/Y/roll offset ($\tau \approx 0.4\text{ s}$) without any visible jump.
