# Ocean 1.10.1 — Multi-stem *Pocillopora* nursery coral, dual reef-slab anemone colonies, and interactive clownfish anemone sheltering

Ocean 1.10.1 refines the foreground coral shelters and anemone habitats on both sides of the reef while keeping all existing settings, fluorescence patterns and frame-rate options unchanged:

- **Three-trunk *Pocillopora* nursery coral & larger *Chromis* schools:**
  - **Broad, fused multi-colony base:** the shelter coral at `[1.35, -4.18, 2.9]` now grows from three closely spaced, organically fused basal trunks into a dense, cauliflower-like *Pocillopora* colony (`size = 1.32`) with verrucae surface micro-relief and wet mucus sheen (`clearCoat = 0.72`).
  - **Double-sized nursery fish:** the two resident *Chromis* damselfish schools (`scale = 0.096`) hover above and dart between the *Pocillopora* branches when startled.
- **Both left and right multi-crown anemone colonies on flat reef-rock slabs:**
  - **Restored right-side anemone colony alongside the left colony:** both the front-left/center colony (`[-0.35, -3.48, 3.05]`, radius `0.62`) and the right colony (`[4.35, -3.8, 0.25]`, radius `0.48`) are present on irregular, flat 2-slab reef rock foundations matching the surrounding reef stone.
  - **Dense 10-crown tentacle coverage:** each reef slab is covered across its full width by 10 closely grown anemone crowns (320 swaying tentacles per colony) moving smoothly with the underwater swell.
- **Proportional clownfish size, natural anemone sheltering & tentacle deflection:**
  - **Natural clownfish proportions & sheltering:** Clownfish (`0.56×` base scale) alternate naturally between nestling among the tentacles of both the left and right anemone colonies and cruising in the water column above them.
  - **Interactive tentacle deflection:** when a Clownfish or Pink Skunk Anemonefish swims through either anemone colony, nearby tentacles bend smoothly aside around the fish's body while their bases remain anchored to the rock.

All existing settings, frame-rate choices (30, 40, 50 and 60 FPS), fish counts and coral layouts remain unchanged. **Phone battery savings, frame rate and temperature have not been measured.**
