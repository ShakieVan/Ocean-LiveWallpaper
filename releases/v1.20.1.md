# Ocean 1.20.1 (Beta) — Less graphics work, the same picture

Ocean 1.20.1 (Beta) removes graphics work whose result you never see. The picture stays the same:

- **Sand is drawn after the reef,** so sand hidden behind the reef is skipped instead of being calculated and painted over.
- **Light patterns are only read where they show:** not outside their area, and not on reef and plant surfaces that face away from the light or lie in full shadow.
- **Leftover calculations are gone** from the reef and fish shaders, and a camera calculation runs only when the camera actually moves.

This should reduce the load on the graphics chip. Its effect on battery life has not been measured yet.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.20.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.20.1).
