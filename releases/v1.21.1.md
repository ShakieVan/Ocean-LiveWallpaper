# Ocean 1.21.1 — Particles and plants in step with the fish

Ocean 1.21.1 fixes the swell for the drifting particles and the plants:

- **Particles move with the fish:** in 1.21.0 the particles rode the swell almost against the fish. They now move with the same water at the same moment, and they no longer stir when the camera moves.
- **Plants sway in step:** the plants had the same offset since the swell was introduced.
- **Clownfish part the anemone tentacles:** the same fix makes the tentacles give way when a clownfish swims through them.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.21.1 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.21.1).
