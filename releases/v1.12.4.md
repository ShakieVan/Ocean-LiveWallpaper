# Ocean 1.12.4 (Beta) — Full 360° 1:1 Z-rotation and zero drift during back-and-forth Z-turns

Ocean 1.12.4 (Beta) completes the 3rd-axis (**Rotate screen**) gyroscope tracking so that full 360° turns rotate the ocean a full 360° (instead of stopping upside down at 180°) and back-and-forth Z-rotations remain free of drift while preserving 100% drift-free Y/X tilting:

- **Unattenuated flat-top smoothstep gate (`[0.60, 0.95]` in `TiltController.advanceRoll`):**
  - In `1.12.3`, the continuous Lorentzian factor $\big(\Delta\theta_z^2 / (\Delta\theta_z^2 + 1.5\,\text{tilt}^2)\big)^2$ attenuated genuine Z-rotations whenever natural hand X/Y wobble accompanied the turn (losing ~50% of the angle over a 360° hand-over-hand turn so the ocean ended up at 180°, and rectifying asymmetric wrist wobble between left and right Z-strokes into drift).
  - `advanceRoll` now uses a flat-top smoothstep gate on $|\Delta\theta_z| / \text{tilt}$ in `[0.60, 0.95]`: below `0.60 * tilt` (covering even the turnaround of a $\pm 28^\circ$ Y-tilt where $\tan 28^\circ \approx 0.53$), weight is `0` so Y/X tilting produces zero horizon drift; at or above `0.95 * tilt`, weight is `1.0` so genuine Z-rotations are 100% unattenuated.
- **Holonomy-free gravity-meridian differential (`dGrav`) in inclined postures:**
  - In non-flat postures, blends body-frame $\Delta\theta_z$ with the exact gravity-meridian differential $\Delta\Phi = \text{atan2}(g_x, g_y)_t - \text{atan2}(g_x, g_y)_{t-1}$ and gently anchors to `gravRoll` during active Z-rotation, eliminating 3D spherical coning holonomy ($\oint \cos\theta \, d\psi$) over both repeated back-and-forth Z-rotations and full 360° revolutions.
