# Ocean 1.4.0 — Texture controls and camera gestures

Version **1.4.0 / code 20**. The public update from 1.3.0 was successfully
installed through Ocean and Android’s installer on September 15, 2026.

- **Fine texture contours:** the new **Detailkonturen** slider strengthens
  small lines within textured surfaces, from 0–100%. It starts off and does
  not draw outlines around whole objects.
- **Colour saturation:** **Farbsättigung** ranges from 0% grayscale through
  the existing colours at 100% to stronger colours at 200%. Default: 100%.
  The fluorescence strength remains a separate saved choice.
- **Texture sharpness:** the existing slider now sharpens local texture
  details on coral, rocks, fish and plants. It remains optional and starts off.
- **Camera gestures in the app preview:** drag with one finger to rotate,
  drag with two to move, and pinch to zoom. A compact camera menu keeps reset
  and advanced controls available. Your saved view also applies to the wallpaper;
  the launcher's gestures and the wallpaper's existing tap response are preserved.
- **Revised coral shapes:** three broad turquoise-orange solitary discs,
  three thin brown-beige coral terraces and slimmer scale columns refine
  existing visual types. The menus retain eight fish types, fourteen reef types
  and 22 model portraits with individual switches.
- **Five giant clams:** two additional clams join the previous three on
  existing reef rock, toward the right foreground and farther back at a higher
  position. Their mantle movement and fluorescent blue bands are preserved.

All nine appearance controls save independently. Camera reset preserves your
appearance and species choices. No extra species selection is needed for the
revised coral shapes or additional clams.

The final Android emulator regression passed all 19 checks, including native
texture effects, saved controls, gestures, repeated surface recreation, resume,
fluorescent clams and fish, and the small schools’ tap response. All 22 model
portraits were regenerated from the final scene. These are emulator observations,
not phone performance or battery measurements.

The update is available through **Settings > Updates**
(`Einstellungen > Updates`) or as **Ocean-1.4.0-universal.apk** on this release.
The interface currently uses German labels. Android supplies the wallpaper
destination picker; available home/lock-screen combinations depend on your phone.

The new shapes and texture processing are original. Existing asset notices
continue to apply. Previous installation results, including earlier Play Protect
blocks, retain their original dates and versions in the
[installation guide](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
