# Ocean 1.10.5

- **Foreground right anemone colony:** Moves the right 10-crown anemone colony and its 2-slab rock foundation forward into the sunlit foreground sand right of the foreground shelter coral (`[4.05, -3.96, 2.50]`) and updates the right clownfish pair's shelter routes and rock-clearance floor accordingly.
- **Corals & Clams menu additions & updated previews:** Adds **Anemone** (`coral_14.png`) as a toggleable 15th species in the **Corals & Clams** submenu (letting clownfish/anemonefish swim freely when disabled) and updates the **Shelter Coral** preview portrait (`coral_5.png`) to show the 3-stem *Pocillopora* colony.
- **Toggleable shelter-coral fish shadows & GPU/CPU efficiency optimisations:** Adds a **Shelter-coral fish shadows** toggle under **Shadows** (defaulting to off), halves anemone mesh triangle count (~35,280 instead of ~69,600 triangles) with outward-facing normals and single-sided hardware backface culling (`doubleSided: false`), disables shadow casting on the animated anemone mesh, streamlines per-pixel water-light floor caustics to a 2-band wave evaluation, and eliminates per-frame array allocations and redundant `sqrt`/`hypot` calls in fish collision avoidance.

Phone battery savings have not been measured.
