# Ocean 1.14.1 (Beta) — Steering roll independent of tilt strength, 1:1 parallax and pivot for the on-site camera

Ocean 1.14.1 (Beta) refines the camera modes introduced in 1.14.0:

- **Steering stays 1:1:** the **Tilt effect** (*Neigungseffekt*) now scales only tilting and turning. Rotating the phone like a steering wheel (**Rotate screen (3rd axis)**) always rolls the camera 1:1, in both camera modes and in any order of movements. At 0 % only the roll remains.
- **Parallax uses the same percentage as the on-site camera:** 100 % is the mirrored phone rotation 1:1 around the pivot. The effect is therefore much stronger than before at the same percentage (previously 100 % was about 22 % of the phone rotation); lower the tilt effect if it feels too strong. **Tilt limits** are now a stop per axis: 100 % ≈ 18°, 500 % ≈ 90° of phone tilt.
- **Pivot distance for both modes:** the new lowest level **0 In der Kamera** turns the camera about itself. Levels 1–5 place the pivot 5 to 15 units ahead; the on-site camera then circles that point 1:1. The default level 3 makes the on-site camera circle a point 9.5 units ahead after updating — choose level 0 for the 1.14.0 behavior. The camera can pass through rocks at larger pivot distances.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.14.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.14.1).
