# Ocean 1.7.1 — Gentler fish fluorescence

The new fish fluorescence in 1.7.0 was too strong at the normal setting.
Version **1.7.1 / code 27** reduces its emitted light to 6% of the previous
strength while keeping the same fine contours, colours and soft inward fades.

At the same scene brightness, **100% now matches the previous 6%** for the
new fish markings. The little neon schools share the correction. Coral, clam
and striped reef fish fluorescence keep their existing calibration.

Your saved slider value is retained. If you reduced it to 6%, set
**Settings > Fluorescence back to 100%** after updating for the new normal
fish strength. This also restores corals and the striped reef fish to their
existing 100% setting. The control still ranges from 0% off to 200% stronger.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from
the [1.7.1 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.7.1).
