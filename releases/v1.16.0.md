# Ocean 1.16.0 (Beta) — Swell rolling over the reef and an adjustable water level

Ocean 1.16.0 (Beta) adds two options under **Light & Water**:

- **Swell** (*Dünung*: Off / Gentle / Strong, default Off): long, slow waves from afar roll over the reef. The water surface rises and falls, bright light bands travel across the reef, plants sway and fish drift along — all in the same rhythm. Works with classic and lively surface waves.
- **Water level** (*Wasserspiegel*): lowers the water surface from the original depth (about 2.3 m above the front reef tops) down to about 0.5 m above them, so the surface appears closer and larger. Light, swell, reef brightness, floating particles and the fishes' swimming height follow. At low levels, tall rocks far in the background can break through the surface.
- Measured on a Galaxy S21 in a test scene: no measurable GPU difference with strong swell. No general battery or FPS claim is made for other phones.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.16.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.16.0).
