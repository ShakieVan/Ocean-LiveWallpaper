# Ocean 1.24.2 (Beta) — Ocean's own signing key

Ocean 1.24.2 (Beta) is the first version signed with Ocean's own release key. Until 1.24.1, Ocean was signed with a general Android development key. The previous key has handed Ocean over to the new one (key rotation of Android's APK Signature Scheme v3), so Android updates an installed Ocean without uninstalling it and keeps your settings. The app itself is the same as 1.24.1.

## How to update

- **From 1.24.1:** through **Settings > Updates**. Ocean 1.24.1 offers this version right after you open it.
- **From 1.24.0 or older:** their updater only accepts the previous key, so it offers **1.24.1** first, which stays marked "Latest" on GitHub for exactly this reason. After that update, open Ocean once more and 1.24.2 is offered. Alternatively, download the APK below and install it directly over your current version: Android accepts the handover and keeps your settings.

## Known limits

- Checked on an API-36 Android emulator, not on phones and not on Android 9 to 12.
- The new certificate has no history with Google Play Protect yet; a scan prompt on your phone is possible.
- The lock screen itself was not looked at. This is a beta.

Download the universal APK from the [1.24.2 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.24.2).
