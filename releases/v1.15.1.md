# Ocean 1.15.1 (Beta) — Lively waves now travel as directional, overlapping wave trains

Ocean 1.15.1 (Beta) reworks the optional **Lively** water-surface waves (**Light & Water > Surface waves**):

- Waves now travel mainly toward the viewer at a slight angle, with more spread for shorter waves and two crossing swells, so you can see them move and overlap instead of the cloud-like swirls of 1.15.0.
- Longer waves travel faster than short ones, as on the open sea.
- Crests are much sharper, troughs stay calm.
- **Classic** waves and the light patterns on the reef are unchanged; Classic remains the default.
- Measured on a Galaxy S21 in a test scene: no measurable GPU difference in the forward view; with the surface filling the screen at most about 0.5 ms more per frame. No general battery or FPS claim is made for other phones.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.15.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.15.1).
