# Ocean 1.17.0 (Beta) — A reef all around the camera

Ocean 1.17.0 (Beta) builds out the scene for the **On-site camera**:

- **Reef all around:** a ring of rocks and corals to the left, right and 3–8 m behind the camera, built from the existing reef models. It is also visible in parallax mode; corals in the ring follow the coral switches.
- **Sand to the horizon:** the sand floor no longer ends in a hard edge behind the camera, and it stays sharp when looking straight down.
- **Fish all around:** with the On-site camera, some fish also swim beside and behind you.
- Works together with **Swell** and **Water level**. At very low water levels (below about 1.2 m above the front reef), single ring rocks behind the camera can break through the surface.
- Measured on a Galaxy S21 in a test scene: the forward view is as fast as before; looking backwards now draws a reef wall instead of empty sand and costs more GPU time, but still less than the forward view. No general battery or FPS claim is made for other phones.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.17.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.17.0).
