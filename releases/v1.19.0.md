# Ocean 1.19.0 (Beta) — Light patterns after a ray-traced reference

For this release the light patterns that the waves cast on the reef and the sand (caustics) were first computed physically on a PC. Ocean 1.19.0 (Beta) now draws them much closer to that reference:

- **Reef tops** show an irregular net of light with bright knots, instead of even, thin lines.
- **The deep sand** gets soft clouds of light that drift across the floor with the waves.
- The pattern **moves with the waves** instead of wobbling in place. Swell and water level still shape it, with water lighting on or off.

On a Galaxy S21 the graphics load stayed within about 1 % of 1.18.2 in all tested views. Phones that cannot render the required texture format keep the previous pattern.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.19.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.19.0).
