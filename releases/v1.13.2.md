# Ocean 1.13.2 (Beta) — Stable horizon when rotating around the vertical Y-axis while holding the phone upright

Ocean 1.13.2 (Beta) fixes a horizon-tilting issue when rotating around the vertical Y-axis past $\sim 45^\circ$ while holding the phone upright or quasi-upright:

- **Gimbal-free upright Y-axis rotation (`TiltController.cameraRoll`):**
  - Previously, extracting relative 3rd-axis roll from row 0 of the relative rotation matrix (`atan2(-r01, r00)`) used an XYZ Euler decomposition with the Y-axis as the middle gimbal axis. Rotating around Y past $\sim 45^\circ$ caused $r_{00} = \cos\theta_y$ to shrink toward zero (reaching Gimbal Lock at $90^\circ$ and flipping $180^\circ$ beyond $90^\circ$), amplifying natural upright inclination offsets or slight wrist pitch changes into an apparent horizon roll ($\operatorname{atan2}(\tan\theta_y \sin\Delta\theta_x, 1)$).
  - In upright and quasi-upright postures, relative roll is now computed directly from the screen-plane gravity projection relative to the calibration baseline, paired with a smooth YXZ turntable fallback for steeper postures and direct angle addition of the baseline horizon roll.
  - Looking around the reef horizontally across the full $\pm 180^\circ$ Y-axis range now keeps the reef horizon level when the phone remains upright, while still tracking true 3rd-axis screen rotation 1:1 (both with **Spirit-level alignment** enabled and disabled).
