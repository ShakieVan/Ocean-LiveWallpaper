# Ocean 1.15.0 (Beta) — Choose between classic and lively water-surface waves

Ocean 1.15.0 (Beta) adds a choice for the water surface seen from below, under **Light & Water**:

- **Surface waves** (*Wellen der Oberfläche*): **Classic** (default, unchanged) or **Lively**. Lively waves sum many overlapping waves with sharp crests and small ripples dragged along by the larger ones, adapted from ["Very fast procedural ocean"](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/MdXyzX) by afl_ext (MIT License). Only the underside of the surface is computed, without raymarching.
- **Wave count (lively)** (*Wellenanzahl*): 5 to 20 overlapping waves, default 8. More waves show finer ripples; ripples finer than a few pixels fade out automatically in the distance. Both settings switch live.
- The light patterns on the reef are unchanged.
- Measured on a Galaxy S21 in a frozen test scene: no measurable GPU difference in the normal forward view; with the surface filling the whole screen (on-site camera looking straight up), 5–12 waves cost the same as or less than classic, 20 waves about 10 % more GPU time. No general battery or FPS claim is made for other phones.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.15.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.15.0).
