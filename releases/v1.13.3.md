# Ocean 1.13.3 (Beta) — Posture-independent horizon stability when rotating around the Y-axis after laying down or standing up

Ocean 1.13.3 (Beta) fixes horizon tilting when rotating around the Y-axis after changing the phone's pitch posture between calibration and rotation (laying the phone down from an upright initialization or standing it up from a horizontal initialization):

- **Posture-independent Y-axis rotation (`TiltController.cameraRoll`):**
  - Because 3D rotations are non-commutative ($\operatorname{rotX}(\pm 90^\circ)\cdot\operatorname{rotY}(\theta_y) \equiv \operatorname{rotZ}(\pm\theta_y)\cdot\operatorname{rotX}(\pm 90^\circ)$), pitching the phone by $\sim 90^\circ$ around X (laying it down or standing it up) before rotating around Y previously caused $r_{22} = \cos\Delta\theta_x\cos\theta_y$ to vanish and coupled the Y-rotation into apparent 3rd-axis Z-roll.
  - `TiltController.cameraRoll` now un-rotates the pure X-pitch posture change $\Delta\theta_x$ between `baseline` and `current` before extracting the YXZ turntable roll, blends directly with division-free `(-r01, r00)`, and gates upright gravity stabilization on current portrait uprightness (`currPlanar` and `gy`) relative to `horizonRoll(baseline)`.
  - Rotating around the Y-axis now keeps the reef horizon level across the full $\pm 180^\circ$ range whether the phone is initialized upright and kept upright, initialized upright and laid flat, initialized flat and kept flat, or initialized flat and stood upright, while preserving 1:1 3rd-axis screen rotation.
