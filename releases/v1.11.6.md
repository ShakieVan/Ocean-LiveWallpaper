# Ocean 1.11.6 — Adjustable tilt limits up to 5× wider gyroscope range

Ocean 1.11.6 adds a new **Tilt limits** (*Neigungsgrenzen*) slider (`100%–500%`) directly below **Tilt strength** so gyroscope camera movement can continue up to five times further when tilting the device beyond a small wrist angle:

- **Configurable gyroscope tilt limits (`100%–500%`, up to 5× range):**
  - **Unchanged sensitivity at small angles:** At `100%` (default), gyroscope parallax clamps at the existing ~18° wrist tilt (`±3.9°` horizontal / `±3.0°` vertical orbit around the chosen pivot). Raising **Tilt limits** up to `500%` keeps the exact same calm sensitivity per degree of tilt near center while opening the clamp up to **5× wider** (`±19.6°` horizontal / `±15.1°` vertical spherical orbit) as you continue tilting the device up to ~90°.
  - **Symmetric gimbal-free vertical angle extraction:** Computes vertical tilt relative to $\sqrt{r_6^2 + r_8^2}$ instead of $r_8$ alone, preventing vertical angle amplification when the device is tilted strongly to the side.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.11.6 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.11.6).
