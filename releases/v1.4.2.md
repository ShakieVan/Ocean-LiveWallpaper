# Ocean 1.4.2 — Clearer fish fin attachments

Version **1.4.2 / code 22** addresses ragged-looking dark marks around pectoral
fins. All eight fish species were examined after the original bannerfish report.

- Local colour corrections remove two confirmed texture defects: the dark mark
  on the bannerfish's white flank and a slit on the blue tang's blue flank.
  Both sides and both model detail levels receive the correction.
- Combining **Texture sharpness** and **Fine texture contours** no longer
  darkens fin attachments as aggressively. Existing colours and stripes remain.
- Gentler ambient occlusion keeps small fin recesses more readable. It also
  slightly brightens deep reef crevices. Directional cast shadows remain active.

The marks had different causes: some were baked into textures, while others
came from the shape and shading of the fin attachment. Meshes, UV mapping,
atlas images and swimming animation are unchanged. No textures, texture samples
or rendering passes are added.

All eight species passed their final native comparisons. The bannerfish and
blue tang also passed 64 fin-cycle poses across both sides and both model detail
levels. All nine final Android regression checks passed, including repeated
surface recreation and resume, texture controls, fluorescence and model previews.
The signed wallpaper also passed four system-preview openings alongside the
active wallpaper, with an empty crash log. All 22 species portraits were refreshed. These are emulator checks, not phone
performance or battery measurements.
