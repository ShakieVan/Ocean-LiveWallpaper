# Ocean 1.8.0 — Moving water light and softer shadows

Two new, independent switches bring a more natural underwater feel:

- **Moving water light** (`Bewegtes Wasserlicht`) adds gently changing light
  directions and broader, moving reflections across fish, small schools,
  corals and plants. Surface shimmer and light rays follow the same waves.
- **Distance-softened shadows** (`Abstandsabhängig weiche Schatten`) keeps
  nearby shadow edges narrow and spreads distant shadows into a softer,
  fainter shade. Enable a shadow quality level for this option to take effect.

Both switches are in **Settings** and are **off by default**, including after
an update. Try them separately or together. Your other settings are retained.

This is a real-time approximation without ray tracing. Shadows follow the
average sunlight direction; broad overhangs can still cast substantial shade.
Both options add graphics work. Their battery and frame-rate impact has not
been measured on a phone, so you can turn either option off independently.

All eight fish types, the four switch combinations, surface recreation,
repeated wallpaper previews beside an active wallpaper, and screen resume
were checked in the native Android renderer on an API-36 emulator.
