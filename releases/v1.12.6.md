# Ocean 1.12.6 (Beta) — Lock-screen 3rd-axis rotation blockade and 2-second post-unlock delay

Ocean 1.12.6 (Beta) replaces the initial wake-up horizon suppression with a complete **3rd-axis rotation blockade** while the lock screen is active (`roll = 0 rad`), followed by a **2-second post-unlock delay** (`POST_UNLOCK_DELAY_NANOS`) after the lock screen is dismissed:

- **Lock-screen rotation blockade (`lockScreenActive`, `TiltController.noteLockOrWakeState`):**
  - While the lock screen is active (`KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked == true` or screen off), 3rd-axis Z-rotation is completely blocked (`trackedRoll = 0f`, `offsetRoll = 0f`, `roll = 0f`, keeping the horizon aligned with the phone display regardless of how long the lock screen stays open).
  - Lock-screen dismissal is detected both via `Intent.ACTION_USER_PRESENT` / `setVisible` in `OceanRenderer` and via a lightweight $100\text{ ms}$ check (`LOCK_POLL_INTERVAL_NANOS`, active only while `lockScreenActive == true`).
- **2-second post-unlock delay (`POST_UNLOCK_DELAY_NANOS = 2_000_000_000L`):**
  - The 2-second countdown starts only after the lock screen ends / the phone is unlocked (`lockScreenActive` transitions to `false`).
  - During those 2 seconds after unlocking, the rotation blockade remains active (`roll = 0f`) so hand adjustments during unlocking do not tilt the horizon.
  - Once the 2 seconds have elapsed, the blockade is released and the horizon smoothly eases ($\tau \approx 0.4\text{ s}$) into spirit-level alignment (`horizonRoll(current)`) and 1:1 gyroscope Z-rotation.
