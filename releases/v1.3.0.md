# Ocean 1.3.0 — Finer reef detail

Reef surfaces now have more detailed relief and crevice shading, with finer
nearby coral shapes and a new blue-violet scale column. Fish skin gains finer,
directional scales and small grooves across all eight fish types.

- **More detailed coral:** finer fan branches, thin irregular plate edges and
  more strongly folded cup and scroll corals. Nearby imported colonies use
  more detailed models on High and Maximum.
- **New scale column:** three original blue-violet colonies with overlapping
  scale-like growths. Find **Schuppensäule** in **Corals & clams**
  (`Korallen & Muscheln`). The menu now contains fourteen reef types, each
  with its own switch and native model portrait.
- **Detail control:** **Detailgrad** in Settings offers Basic, Fine, High and
  Maximum. Basic and Fine use simpler imported coral models; High and Maximum
  show finer shapes and surface relief. The default remains High.
- **Saved choices:** existing individual exclusions are preserved. If every
  previously available reef type was enabled, the new column is enabled too.
- **Appearance controls:** texture sharpness, brightness, contrast, water
  clarity, reef colour and independent fluorescence remain adjustable.

Use **Settings > Updates** (`Einstellungen > Updates`) in Ocean, or download
**Ocean-1.3.0-universal.apk** from this release. The interface is currently German.
Android supplies the wallpaper destination picker; available home/lock-screen
combinations depend on your phone.

Higher detail uses more graphics resources. Actual performance and battery
use depend on your phone and settings; this release does not claim a measured
phone battery result or a numerical detail multiplier over another app.

The new geometry and scale patterns are original. No images or assets from
the reference app are packaged in Ocean. Existing asset notices continue to apply.

Verified on an Android API-36 emulator: all 22 model portraits, individual
species choices, appearance and fluorescence controls, repeated activity and
surface recreation, screen off/on, and four system previews while Ocean was
already the active home-screen wallpaper. These checks do not establish phone
performance or battery savings.

A fresh API-36 emulator also successfully updated from 1.2.1 to this release
through Ocean's public updater and Android's installer. No scan or block dialog
appeared. Ocean reopened normally, the crash log was empty and the scoped
installation permission was switched off again. [Installation evidence](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
