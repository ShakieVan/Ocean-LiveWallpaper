# Ocean 1.11.0 — Bulbous-tip anemones with clownfish pairs, 3-stem Pocillopora shelter coral, fish-to-fish collision avoidance, wider camera rotation and sub-10%/h shadow/shader efficiency

Ocean 1.11.0 introduces bulbous-tip (*Entacmaea*) sea anemone colonies with resident clownfish pairs and interactive tentacle deflection, a redesigned 3-stem *Pocillopora* shelter coral with larger resident *Chromis* schools, 3D fish-to-fish collision avoidance, wider horizontal camera rotation ($\pm 55^\circ$), and major shadow-pipeline and shader efficiency optimisations that bring real-device battery consumption below 10%/h on a Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra:

- **Bulbous-tip sea anemones (*Entacmaea*) & resident clownfish pairs:**
  - **Left & foreground-right 10-crown colonies:** Two 10-crown anemone colonies on flat 2-slab reef rock foundations (`[-0.35, -3.48, 3.05]` on the left and `[3.30, -3.96, 2.50]` in the sunlit foreground sand right of the foreground shelter coral).
  - **Natural bulbous-tip silhouette & warm colouration:** Cylindrical tentacle stalks with rounded bulbous dome tips (`Knöllchen` / eye-stalk silhouette) in soft rose and warm beige tones without artificial bluish specular sheen (`OCEAN_WATER_DIFFUSE_ONLY`, `clearCoat = 0.0`, `reflectance = 0.10`).
  - **Clownfish pairs, cuddling & interactive tentacle deflection:** Doubled clownfish count as female/male pairs (`0.66×` and `0.56×` already included at the default 16-fish setting) that cuddle through the upper/mid tentacle crowns with exact flat-plateau rock clearance (`anemoneRockFloor`), deflecting up to 4 swimmers' surrounding tentacles smoothly in the vertex shader, and periodically swim across the foreground camera view (`2.15–2.70 m`) when not sheltering.
  - **Corals & Clams submenu control:** **Anemone** (`coral_14.png`) is available as a toggleable 15th species under **Corals & Clams**; when turned off, the anemone colonies hide and clownfish/anemonefish switch to free reef swimming.
- **3-stem *Pocillopora* shelter coral & larger *Chromis* schools:**
  - **Multi-stem *Pocillopora* architecture:** Three thick, organically branching stems growing from a broad fused base with verrucae micro-relief and wet mucus sheen (`clearCoat = 0.72`), plus an updated **Shelter Coral** preview portrait (`coral_5.png`) in the **Corals & Clams** menu.
  - **Double-sized resident *Chromis* damselfish:** Resident shelter-coral fish are twice as large for clear visibility while keeping their dart-and-hide response inside the coral branches.
- **3D fish-to-fish collision avoidance & exact cliff clearance:**
  - **Proactive 3D steering & pairwise separation:** Fish steer smoothly around approaching neighbours (`avoidFish`) and maintain non-penetrating body separation (`resolveFishOverlap`) so fish no longer swim through one another.
  - **Continuous right-cliff collider (`rock 2`):** Matches the actual right-hand rock face (`x <= 3.75`) so non-shelter fish such as the blue Regal Tang never get stuck or clip into the rock beside the right anemone.
- **Wider horizontal camera rotation ($\pm 55^\circ$):**
  - Expands the horizontal camera yaw range by $30^\circ$ on both sides from $\pm 25^\circ$ to $\pm 55^\circ$ (`MAX_CAMERA_YAW = 55`) for both one-finger touch gestures and the **Camera settings** slider.
- **Shadow-pipeline, shader & GPU/CPU efficiency (< 10%/h verified on Galaxy S24 Ultra):**
  - **Elimination of duplicate EVSSM work & 21 per-frame Gaussian mipmap passes:** When precomputed $2048 \times 2048$ soft EVSSM coral shadows (`StaticReefShadow`) are active, dynamic cascades (which hold only moving fish and swaying plants) now use hardware `DEPTH16` `PCF` at $1024 \times 1024$ instead of `DPCF`/`PCSS` (`RGBA16F` moment maps), eliminating 21 full-screen Gaussian mipmap passes per frame and a second per-pixel 5-tap EVSSM evaluation while keeping soft precomputed reef shadows intact.
  - **Single-tap contact shadows & toggleable shelter-fish shadows:** `static-shadow.glsl` uses 1 hardware bilinear `textureLod` tap instead of 5 Quincunx taps when penumbra width is within 1.25 texels; anemone meshes (`35,280` single-sided triangles with hardware backface culling) do not cast shadows; and a new **Shelter-coral fish shadows** toggle under **Shadows** defaults to off.
  - **Reef, water-light & fish shader pruning:** Raises the triplanar weight cutoff in `reef.mat` from `0.03` to `0.10` (skipping secondary/tertiary `textureGrad` array fetches on ~80% of reef surfaces), skips `animatedMapFrame()` on flat/distant normals, streamlines per-pixel water-light floor caustics to a 2-band wave model, removes an unused `textureLod` face lookup in `fish.mat`, and gates 4-tap neighbour sharpening, gill breathing and fluorescence lookups to active/near-detail fish.
  - **Halved background shader compilation & hardware upscaling:** Skips loading unused non-waterlight `.filamat` packages on startup, compiles only active `shadowReceiver` material states, uses hardware bilinear scaling (`QualityLevel.LOW`) when `renderScalePercent < 100`, removes `FLAG_DIM_BEHIND` on the settings sheet, and uses zero-allocation squared-distance pre-filters in the fish simulation loop.
