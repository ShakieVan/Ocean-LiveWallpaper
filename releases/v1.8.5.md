# Ocean 1.8.5 — More efficient soft shadows

Distance-softened shadows now do less repeated work. Ocean groups shadow-map
preparation, uses a more efficient blur filter with the same weights, and
precomputes values shared by pixels in each shadow region.

Shadow resolution, quality levels, contact edges and distance-based softness
remain unchanged. Your existing settings are preserved; no new switch is needed.
Moving caustics, fish reflections and the reef lighting correction from 1.8.4
are retained.

Controlled native emulator comparisons preserved the measured shadow widths
and fading. All 24 isolated fish images and the fixed scenes with hard shadows
or no shadows match the previous version pixel for pixel. Soft shadows have
small GPU rounding differences. Overall emulator GPU timings were similar;
this comparison did not establish a reliable speedup. Performance and battery
savings on a phone have not been measured.

Native lifecycle and surface-recreation checks passed. The signed release also
passed four system previews alongside an active Ocean wallpaper and a
screen-off/resume check on an Android emulator.

The public update from 1.8.4 to 1.8.5 passed through Ocean and Android's installer
on the dedicated API-36 emulator. Lighting options, 40% caustic speed, 30 FPS,
shadow quality and fish/growth settings were retained. The installed APK matches
the public download; temporary installation permission was turned off again.
No crash, ANR, scan or block dialog was observed in this update test. This is
not a phone test or general Play Protect clearance.
