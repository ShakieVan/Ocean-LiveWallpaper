# Ocean 1.17.2 (Beta) — A calm picture when switching on in motion

Ocean 1.17.2 (Beta) improves what you see right after switching the screen on:

- **Switched on while moving** (for example taking the phone out of a pocket): as long as the phone is still being turned noticeably, the view stays on the saved framing, aligned to the display. Once you hold the phone calmly for about 0.3 seconds, that posture becomes the reference and looking around starts — without a jump.
- **Switched on at rest** (for example picked up from the table first): unchanged — the view is aligned to the display and follows your movements after a moment.
- The lock screen behaves as in 1.17.1.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.17.2 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.17.2).
