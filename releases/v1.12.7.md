# Ocean 1.12.7 (Beta) — Full X/Y/Z reset at the end of the 2-second post-unlock delay

Ocean 1.12.7 (Beta) completes the 2-second post-unlock workflow introduced in 1.12.6 so that a full, responsive **Reset** of both X/Y tilt (`baseline`) and 3rd-axis spirit-level horizon (`horizonRoll`) executes right at the end of the 2-second post-unlock delay:

- **Shared post-unlock state across engines (`sharedLockScreenActive`, `sharedUnlockPending`, `sharedUnlockStartNanos`):**
  - Synchronizes lock-screen and post-unlock countdown state across all `TiltController` instances in the process and detects keyguard dismissal in `OceanRenderer.setVisible`, ensuring the 2-second post-unlock countdown survives dual-engine (`LockScreenEngine` $\to$ `HomeScreenEngine`) transitions and visibility re-calibrations.
- **Explicit Reset at the end of the 2 seconds (`POST_UNLOCK_RESET_DECAY_RATE = 6.0f`, $\tau \approx 0.17\text{ s}$):**
  - When the 2-second post-unlock delay elapses, `TiltController` resets the X/Y tilt baseline (`baseline`), re-centers the 5-second quiet anchor (`quietAnchor`), and aligns the 3rd-axis horizon (`horizonRoll(current)`), smoothly easing into the new neutral posture with a responsive $\tau \approx 0.17\text{ s}$ transition.
- **True 1:1 spirit-level horizon without portrait deadzone suppression:**
  - Removes the artificial $8^\circ\text{–}18^\circ$ portrait deadzone from `horizonRoll`, so spirit-level alignment corrects small and moderate tilts ($5^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$) 1:1 in all non-flat postures (`planar > FLAT_THRESHOLD`).
