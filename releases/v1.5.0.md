# Ocean 1.5.0 — Camera confirmation and balanced coral layouts

Version **1.5.0 / code 23** changes when a camera adjustment reaches the active
live wallpaper. Drag, pinch and the camera sliders change Ocean's app view and
Android's wallpaper preview. The current wallpaper keeps its old angle until
you confirm **Set wallpaper** in Android's picker. Canceling the picker keeps
the prior active angle while your draft remains in Ocean.

The coral menu now offers **Redistribute corals**. Each press chooses a different
one of four prepared, evenly spread layouts. Every coral form has colonies on
both outer reef sides, with five colonies per form across the scene. All five
giant clams stay in their existing places. The organ pipes have clearer tube
openings; scroll coral has shorter folded layers; scale columns and branching
antler coral are easier to recognise. All 22 species portraits have been
captured again from the current Android models, with more typical views for
several coral types including fleshy coral.

The four layouts were checked against reef bounds and colony spacing. Android
emulator images confirmed a visible change on the outer left and right sides.
An API-36 emulator also confirmed that canceling the system picker preserved
the active angle and accepting it committed the draft. Scene, native preview,
lint and automated tests passed. These are emulator observations, not phone
battery or performance measurements. Existing asset origins and license terms
remain in [Third-Party Notices](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Update in Ocean under **Settings > Updates** (`Einstellungen > Updates`).
