# Ocean 1.20.2 (Beta) — Lighter young fish

Ocean 1.20.2 (Beta) makes the tiny young fish around the shelter corals much less work to draw. They look practically the same:

- **A simpler model:** 450 instead of 3,500 triangles per fish. Each fish covers only a small spot on the screen, so the shape holds up at that size.
- **Simpler light:** plain sunlight without light patterns or reflections. They still fall into shadow when they hide inside their coral.
- **Less unused work:** young fish that are not shown skip their swimming calculation, and a few calculations without a visible result are gone from the plants and the light patterns.

Its effect on battery life has not been measured yet.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.20.2 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.20.2).
