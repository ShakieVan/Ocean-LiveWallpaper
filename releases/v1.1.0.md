# Ocean 1.1.0 — Reef detail and appearance controls

More detailed reef surfaces and fish scales, with six new controls to shape your aquarium:

- **Surface detail:** Basic, Fine, High or Maximum. The default is High.
- **Texture sharpness:** bring out existing fine texture contrast; off by default.
- **Brightness:** 30–150%. Lower brightness brings out selected fluorescent coral colours.
- **Contrast:** 50–150%, with the existing appearance at 100%.
- **Water clarity:** reduce visibility for a hazier underwater scene. Maximum retains the previous clear-water view.
- **Reef colour:** move from pale limestone through dark grey to cool magma black. Coral colours stay separate.

The new settings are saved and apply to the preview and live wallpaper. Existing camera, fish and motion settings remain available, including exactly 30, 40, 50 or 60 FPS. Higher detail and sharpness can increase graphics work; actual performance and battery use depend on your device.

Install **Ocean-1.1.0-universal.apk**, or open **Settings > Updates** in Ocean. Android still supplies the wallpaper destination picker, so available home/lock-screen combinations depend on your phone. The app interface is currently German; see the [appearance control guide](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#appearance-controls).
