# Ocean 1.13.0 — Docked Liquid-Glass register selection wheel, 3rd gyroscope axis with spirit-level horizon, landscape tilt-axis alignment, post-unlock full reset, and background update downloads

Ocean 1.13.0 consolidates all improvements since 1.12.0 (`1.12.1`–`1.12.10` Beta) into a major update featuring a right-edge **Liquid-Glass register selection wheel** (*Auswahlrad*) with a central left-edge icon bulge and a bottom-center **Set wallpaper** icon button, a dedicated **Tilt & Gyroscope** submenu with the **3rd gyroscope axis ("Rotate screen")**, 1:1 spirit-level horizon alignment, landscape/upside-down X/Y tilt-axis rotation, lock-screen rotation blockade with a 2-second post-unlock full X/Y/Z reset, and background update downloads across app switches:

- **Right-edge Liquid-Glass register selection wheel & bottom-center wallpaper icon button:**
  - **Docked at the right screen edge with a central left-edge icon bulge (`R.id.settings_open`):** When collapsed, only ~1 mm of the left menu border peeks into the screen on the right edge while the central Liquid-Glass bulge with the settings icon protrudes cleanly into the view. Open the wheel by **pulling left** (with live finger tracking) or **tapping** the bulge; collapse it again by **tapping outside the menu**, swiping right, or pulling/tapping the bulge.
  - **Register wheel, custom vector pictograms & horizontal submenu transitions:** Entries scroll vertically through a fixed central selection zone, smoothly unfolding like register cards (`RegisterCardView`) with custom vector pictograms (`PictogramDrawable`) while curving, scaling and fading toward the top and bottom edges so the live 3D reef stays visible while adjusting settings. Submenus (*Tilt & Gyroscope*, *Fish & Motion*, *Reef & Growth*, *Light & Water*, *Surface & Colours*, *Energy & Expert*) slide in horizontally from the right and return via `< Back` or a right-swipe.
  - **Bottom-center Liquid-Glass "Set wallpaper" button:** Replaces the dark bottom action bar with a circular Liquid-Glass icon button (`LiquidGlassWallpaperButton`) at the bottom center of the screen (coral redistribution remains directly accessible with live preview under **Reef & Growth > Redistribute corals**).
- **3rd gyroscope axis ("Rotate screen"), spirit-level horizon & "Tilt & Gyroscope" submenu:**
  - **Dedicated Tilt & Gyroscope submenu:** Consolidates **Tilt strength**, **Tilt limits** (`100%–500%`), **Pivot distance** (`1–5`), **Invert tilt direction**, **Rotate screen (3rd axis)**, **Invert rotation direction**, and **Tilt response** (*power-saving* / *normal* / *fast*), and restores the original default tilt direction (`-inputX` horizontal, `+inputY` vertical when **Invert tilt direction** is off).
  - **3rd gyroscope axis (`tiltRoll`, default off) & separate Invert rotation direction (`invertTiltRoll`, default off):**
    - Uses the true 1:1 rotation angle ($\operatorname{atan2}$) around the camera view axis (`upX`, `upY`, `upZ`) across full $360^\circ$ rotations (never flipping at $180^\circ$), synchronized with touch projection for the shelter-coral fish schools (`NurserySchools.project`).
    - **1:1 spirit-level (*Wasserwaage*) horizon & flat-screen exception:** Aligns the ocean horizon 1:1 with gravity in upright and tilted postures (`planar > FLAT_THRESHOLD`), while aligning with the phone screen (`0 rad`) when the device lies nearly flat ($\sqrt{g_x^2 + g_y^2} \le 0.16$).
    - **Drift-free 3D sensor math even during simultaneous tilting and rotation (`advanceRoll`):** Combines allocation-free incremental column dot products (`decoupledRollStep`) and a flat-posture `[0.60, 0.95]` smoothstep gate on `dz * weight * (1f - tiltBlend)` with the un-gated, holonomy-free gravity-meridian differential `dGrav * tiltBlend` in non-flat postures, keeping X/Y tilting, back-and-forth Z-rotation, and simultaneous X/Y tilting + Z-rotation completely free of horizon skew or drift.
    - **Horizon-rotated X/Y tilt axes (`ParallaxCamera.update`):** Rotates the phone's X/Y tilt vector `(-inputX, inputY)` by the active horizon roll angle (reusing `cos(roll)` and `sin(roll)` from `updateUpVector` with zero extra trig calls), so pitching a landscape-held or upside-down phone forward/backward always tilts the ocean camera vertically (up/down) relative to the horizon.
    - **Lock-screen rotation blockade, 2-second post-unlock full reset & 5-second quiet re-alignment:** Blocks 3rd-axis rotation (`roll = 0 rad`) while the lock screen is active (`KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked`), waits 2 seconds after unlocking (`POST_UNLOCK_DELAY_NANOS`, synchronized across lock/home engine handoffs), and then executes a smooth ($\tau \approx 0.17\text{ s}$) full reset of X/Y tilt (`baseline`) and 1:1 spirit-level horizon (`horizonRoll`), plus gentle automatic re-alignment ($\tau \approx 0.4\text{ s}$) after **5 seconds of quiet holding** (`isQuiet`, ignoring tremors within $5^\circ$ and brief jolts under $250\text{ ms}$).
- **Background update downloads & immediate install offer:**
  - Ongoing update downloads continue running in the background when switching to another app (`MainActivity.onStop()`) or closing the dialog, automatically verifying the APK signature and SHA-256 checksum and immediately offering **Install update** (`Bereit · <Version>`) in the settings wheel upon returning to Ocean.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.13.0 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.13.0).
