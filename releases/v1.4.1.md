# Ocean 1.4.1 — Tunnel clams and stronger texture controls

Version **1.4.1 / code 21**. The signed release passed its final scene and
wallpaper checks, and the public update from 1.4.0 installed successfully.

- The two clams added in 1.4.0 move to the far-right tunnel view: one on the
  front wall and one on the higher rear rock. There are still five clams in total.
- **Texture sharpness** now has a clearly stronger maximum across the reef,
  including the middle-distance surfaces that previously changed very little.
- **Fine texture contours** now emphasize existing dark texture lines more
  strongly. High values deliberately produce a pronounced effect.
- Both controls keep their 0–100% range and saved settings. Zero remains off;
  intermediate values let you choose a subtler appearance. Fluorescence remains
  separately adjustable. The filter adds no new textures or rendering passes.

Update through **Settings > Updates** (`Einstellungen > Updates`). Camera
gestures, individual species choices and the original three clams are preserved.

All 12 final Android checks passed, including the complete tunnel comparison
at normal and reduced brightness, saved controls, surface recreation and resume,
and clam/fish fluorescence. All 22 model portraits were regenerated and checked.
The signed app passed four repeated system previews while its wallpaper was active.
These are emulator checks, not phone performance or battery measurements.

See the [unchanged native comparison images](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#texture-comparison-in-141).

On September 15, 2026, a fresh API-36 emulator received original 1.4.0
as the starting version, then updated to **1.4.1 / code 21** through Ocean's
public updater and Android's system installer. The download was verified,
installation succeeded and Ocean reopened with an empty crash log. No scan or
block dialog appeared. Ocean's installation permission was switched off again.
This is an emulator result, not phone testing or general Play Protect clearance.
