# Ocean 1.11.7 — Optional inverted gyroscope tilt direction

Ocean 1.11.7 adds an **Invert tilt direction** (*Neigungsrichtung umkehren*) switch directly below the gyroscope controls in **Settings**:

- **Invert tilt direction (`invertTilt`, default off):**
  - Reverses both horizontal and vertical gyroscope camera orbit directions around the selected pivot distance while keeping the chosen **Tilt strength**, **Tilt limits** (`100%–500%`) and **Tilt response** smoothing unchanged.
  - Smoothly transitions to the inverted orbit without jumps when toggled live.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.11.7 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.11.7).
