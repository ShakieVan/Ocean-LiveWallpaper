# Ocean 1.21.2 — How it all began

Ocean 1.21.2 hides a small look back in the app:

- **Tap the installed version five times** under **Settings > Updates**. A short clip shows the very first build of Ocean from September 11, 2026 next to 1.21.1, eight weeks later.
- **Watch the full video:** a button opens the [then-and-now video on YouTube](https://youtu.be/QImZImsj_bY).
- The clip is part of the app and plays without sound or an internet connection. Nothing else changes: the reef, the fish, your settings and the updater stay the same.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.21.2 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.21.2).
