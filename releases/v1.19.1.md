# Ocean 1.19.1 (Beta) — Light patterns on the fish, smoother fins

- **Fish, plants, anemones and young fish** now carry the same soft, drifting light patterns as the reef and the sand from 1.19.0. With water lighting on they previously still showed the old fine net of lines; with it off they had none.
- **Smoother fish:** a small twitch every few seconds to minutes per fish is gone — the forward surge, the hover roll and the gill rhythm no longer jump when a fish's swim cycle starts over. Fluid motion settings between 1 % and 99 % no longer make the fin stroke skip.

On a Galaxy S21 the graphics load stayed unchanged within measurement noise, also with 40 fish.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.19.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.19.1).
