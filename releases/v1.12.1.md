# Ocean 1.12.1 (Beta) — 3rd gyroscope axis with spirit-level horizon alignment, restored default tilt direction, and Liquid-Glass register wheel menu

Ocean 1.12.1 (Beta) introduces the 3rd gyroscope axis (**Rotate screen**) with true 1:1 rotation angle, automatic spirit-level horizon alignment and flat-device screen alignment, restores the original default tilt direction, groups all tilt settings into a dedicated submenu, and replaces the full-screen settings dialog with a right-edge **Liquid-Glass scrollable register wheel**:

- **Restored default tilt direction (`invertTilt`, default off):**
  - Restores the original default horizontal and vertical gyroscope tilt direction (`-inputX` horizontal, `+inputY` vertical when **Invert tilt direction** is off).
- **3rd gyroscope axis ("Rotate screen (3rd axis)") with spirit-level horizon & flat-screen alignment:**
  - **Independent 3rd-axis switch (`tiltRoll`, default off) & dedicated inversion switch (`invertTiltRoll`, default off):** Enables screen-plane gyroscope rotation around the camera view axis with its own **Invert rotation direction** (*Drehrichtung umkehren*) switch, completely independent of **Invert tilt direction** and active even when horizontal/vertical **Tilt strength** is set to `Off`.
  - **True 1:1 rotation angle:** Uses the exact 1:1 physical rotation angle in radians ($\operatorname{atan2}$) around the camera view axis (`upX`, `upY`, `upZ`) and synchronises touch-to-world projection for the interactive shelter-coral fish schools.
  - **Automatic spirit-level (*Wasserwaage*) horizon alignment & flat-device exception:** When the wallpaper becomes visible again and resets X/Y tilt to the current hand posture, the 3rd axis automatically aligns the ocean horizon like a spirit level using the in-plane gravity vector ($\operatorname{atan2}(g_x, g_y)$). When the phone lies nearly flat ($\sqrt{g_x^2 + g_y^2} \le 0.16$, below ~9° tilt) where gravity direction in the screen plane is ambiguous, the horizon smoothly aligns with the screen (`0 rad`) via cubic Hermite interpolation (`smoothstep` between `0.16` and `0.35`).
- **Dedicated "Tilt & Gyroscope" submenu & new right-edge Liquid-Glass register wheel (*Auswahlrad*):**
  - **Consolidated Tilt submenu:** Groups **Tilt strength**, **Tilt limits** (`100%–500%`), **Pivot distance** (`1–5`), **Invert tilt direction**, **Rotate screen (3rd axis)**, **Invert rotation direction**, and **Tilt response** (*power-saving* / *normal* / *fast*) in a single submenu.
  - **Translucent Liquid-Glass register wheel on the right edge:** Leaves the live 3D coral reef visible across the screen while adjusting settings. Items feature custom vector pictograms and a fixed central selection zone (*fester Auswahlbereich*), smoothly folding open and shut like a register tab as they pass through the selection zone while curving, scaling and fading with distance.
  - **Horizontal submenu slide & swipe gestures:** Tapping a submenu slides the current wheel out to the left while the submenu wheel slides in from the right. Tapping the top **`< Back`** (*`< Zurück`*) item or swiping right slides the parent wheel back in from the left; swiping right or tapping outside the top-level wheel collapses it smoothly into the bottom-right settings icon.
