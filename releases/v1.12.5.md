# Ocean 1.12.5 (Beta) — 5-second phone-aligned horizon grace period on wake-up

Ocean 1.12.5 (Beta) disables automatic spirit-level horizon detection for **5 seconds** right after the phone wakes up, aligning the horizon initially with the phone screen (`0 rad`) before smoothly transitioning to spirit-level alignment:

- **5-second wake-up grace window (`TiltController.notePhoneWakeUp`, `WAKE_HORIZON_DELAY_NANOS`):**
  - When waking the phone (`Intent.ACTION_SCREEN_ON`, `PowerManager.isInteractive` transition, or `KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked` lock/unlock transition), `OceanRenderer` signals `tilt.notePhoneWakeUp()`.
  - During the first **5 seconds** (`WAKE_HORIZON_DELAY_NANOS = 5_000_000_000L`), automatic spirit-level horizon detection (`horizonRoll` and gravity-lock pull) is disabled and the horizon aligns directly with the phone display (`0 rad`), including across lock-screen-to-home-screen unlock transitions within that 5-second window.
  - Once 5 seconds have elapsed, spirit-level detection activates and smoothly eases the horizon to `horizonRoll(current)` ($\tau \approx 0.4\text{ s}$) without any jump.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.12.5 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.12.5).
