# Ocean 1.12.9 (Beta) — Drift-free horizon when tilting and rotating around Z simultaneously

Ocean 1.12.9 (Beta) fixes horizon skew when tilting the phone around X/Y while simultaneously rotating it around Z (`TiltController.advanceRoll`):

- **Un-gated gravity-meridian tracking during combined tilt + Z-rotation (`TiltController.advanceRoll`):**
  - Previously, the `[0.60, 0.95]` `|dz|/tilt` gate scaled the entire step `(dz + (dGrav - dz) * tiltBlend) * weight` and gated the `gravRoll` pull to `absDz > 1.0f * tilt`. When tilting and rotating simultaneously (`tilt >= absDz`), part of `dGrav` was discarded or half-wave rectified between tilt-in and tilt-out, leaving the horizon crooked after the turn.
  - The `weight` gate now applies strictly to the flat-posture gyroscope term `dz * weight * (1f - tiltBlend)`, while the holonomy-free gravity differential `dGrav * tiltBlend` remains un-gated in non-flat postures and `pull = ((absDz + tilt) * 2.0f).coerceAtMost(0.35f) * tiltBlend` smoothly locks to `gravRoll` across any combined tilt + rotation motion.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.12.9 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.12.9).
