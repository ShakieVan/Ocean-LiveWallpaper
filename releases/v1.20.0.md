# Ocean 1.20.0 (Beta) — Calm light patterns, a real water light switch, a freer camera

- **No more flicker:** the light patterns on reef and sand no longer shimmer from frame to frame.
- **"Moving water light" means something again:** switched off, reef, sand, fish and plants get even light without light patterns, and the light pattern calculation stops. With swell, the reef keeps its broad, gentle light bands. Switched on, you get the patterns, light tilt and reflections.
- **New defaults for new installations:** on-site camera, lively waves with swell, a lower water level, water light on, 30 FPS, 20 fish and a refined picture. If you have saved settings before, they stay as they are.
- **Freer camera framing:** in the camera editor the view now turns all the way round and almost straight up and down. Moving the camera and zoom keep their limits. If the view turns far away from the main reef, the free-swimming fish follow.
- **Less processor work:** fish look up the reef volumes in a grid, and unneeded recalculations are gone. Do not expect a visibly longer battery life from this yet; the battery work continues.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.20.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.20.0).
