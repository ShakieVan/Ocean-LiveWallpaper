# Ocean 1.11.5 — Visible normal-distance coral, rock, shelter-coral and fish detail

Ocean 1.11.5 fixes the distance-fade and mip-selection thresholds that previously hid fine surface detail at the normal portrait camera view, and connects the **Texture sharpness** and **Detail contours** controls directly to reef pores, foreground shelter corals and fish scales—with zero additional texture memory or sample cost and full **Rock colour** slider support:

- **Full-visibility reef pores and micro-normals at normal viewing distance:**
  - **Corrected world-footprint fade and sharper mip selection:** Widens the triplanar pore and micro-normal distance gates (`smoothstep(42, 110, footprint)` and `smoothstep(18, 48, footprint)`) and biases `textureGrad` derivatives (`0.55×`) so doubling pore density no longer pushes normal portrait views into the fade-out zone or coarse mip levels.
  - **Direct slider coupling on photo-mapped rocks and corals:** Connects **Texture sharpness** and **Detail contours** directly to the triplanar pore/ridge micro-contrast and 16-ray crevice ambient occlusion on photo-mapped rocks and corals (`plate`, `brain`, `sponge`, `fan`, `rock`, `tissue`), instead of only filtering the smooth low-frequency colour atlas.
- **Crisp foreground branching shelter corals and non-photo reef variety:**
  - **Restored shelter-coral (*Pocillopora*) relief and sharpness:** Raises normal-map relief (`0.15 → 0.58`), polyp contrast and **Texture sharpness / Detail contours** response (`10% → 78%`) on the large foreground branching shelter corals while retaining their glossy mucus coat.
  - **Stronger normal and crevice relief across procedural and variety corals:** Uses unscaled UV derivatives (`fwidth(getUV0())`) for normal-map distance fading and applies surface ambient occlusion across non-photo corals, sea plants and rock formations while keeping the **Rock colour** slider unchanged.
- **Stronger mid-distance fish scale definition and outlines:**
  - Extends fish scale resolution into normal canyon swimming distances and strengthens scale-pocket contrast (`±28%`) and scale-lip contour darkening when **Texture sharpness** and **Detail contours** are enabled.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.11.5 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.11.5).
