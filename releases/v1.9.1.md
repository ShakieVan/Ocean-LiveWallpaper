# Ocean 1.9.1 — Less work per frame, same picture

Ocean 1.9.1 does less work for the same picture:

- **Shared surface-detail frame:** fish and coral normal-map details now prepare
  their per-pixel orientation data once instead of up to three times.
- **No discarded calculations:** coral fluorescence only samples its fine
  outline where the pigment can glow at all; procedural patterns that the
  texture maps replace are no longer computed for reef, plants and fish.
- **Fewer engine calls per fish:** each fish's swimming pose is sent to the
  graphics engine in three instead of six updates per frame.
- **Shorter collision checks:** fish route checks stop early for distant reef
  obstacles, with bit-identical swimming paths.

No setting, texture, model, light, shadow method, resolution, fish count or
frame-rate option changed.

On an Android emulator, 36 frozen views covering all eight fish species, three
reef cameras, water light, soft shadows, fluorescence and texture controls were
compared with 1.9.0. Without edge smoothing, no pixel differs by more than one
colour level (shader rounding). With the normal edge smoothing, 0.012% of pixels
differ by more along a few edges. Native lifecycle, surface-recreation and
screen-off/resume checks passed, as did system previews alongside an active Ocean
wallpaper. **Battery use, frame rate and temperature on phones have not been
measured**; emulator timings do not establish a saving.

The public update from 1.9.0 to 1.9.1 passed through Ocean and Android's installer
on an API-36 emulator that first received the public 1.9.0 APK. Water light, soft
shadows, 30 FPS and the shadow quality were retained. The installed APK matches
the public download; temporary installation permission was turned off again
through Ocean's reminder. No crash, ANR, scan or block dialog was observed. The
usual dedicated test emulator reported "Out of space" for the installation; that
is a limit of its 6 GB virtual storage, not of the APK. This is not a phone test
or general Play Protect clearance.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the
