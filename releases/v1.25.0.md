# Ocean 1.25.0 (Beta) — A normal swell

Ocean 1.25.0 (Beta) adds a level to the swell, the long, slow waves that roll over the reef:

- **Normal:** a new level between gentle and strong, at three quarters of the strong swell. Gentle stays at half. The water surface, the bands of light on the reef, the sun rays, plants, fish and suspended particles all follow the chosen level together.
- **A slider instead of buttons:** the setting "Dünung" under "Licht & Wasser" (Light & Water) is now a slider with four named levels: off, gentle, normal, strong. Four buttons did not fit beside each other.
- **Your setting stays:** a swell you saved earlier is unchanged by the update. New installations still start with the strong swell.

Reef, fish, wave styles, updater and signing key are unchanged.

## How to update

- **From 1.24.1 or 1.24.2:** through **Settings > Updates**.
- **From 1.24.0 or older:** their updater offers **1.24.1** first, which stays marked "Latest" on GitHub for this reason. After that update, open Ocean once more and 1.25.0 is offered. Alternatively, download the APK below and install it directly over your current version: Android keeps your settings.

## Known limits

- Checked on an API-36 Android emulator, not on phones. Battery use was not measured.
- The lock screen itself was not looked at. This is a beta.

Download the universal APK from the [1.25.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.25.0).
