# Ocean 1.11.1 — Size-aware shelter-coral collision avoidance for large fish

Ocean 1.11.1 separates large-fish obstacle avoidance around the shelter corals (*Pocillopora*) from small-fish parameters so large species (Butterflyfish, Regal Tang and Pennant Coralfish) glide smoothly over and around the shelter corals without getting stuck, orbiting or bouncing:

- **Dedicated large-fish vs. shelter-coral avoidance:**
  - **Spherical shelter-coral & foundation colliders:** Replaces the flattened ellipsoidal proxy volumes on the foreground and left shelter corals and anemone rock bases with isotropic spherical colliders that enclose the upper coral branch tips without inflating horizontal collision radii for large fish.
  - **Size-aware lookahead & upward shelter-coral lift:** Large fish use a wider lookahead horizon, larger safety padding and an upward-biased repulsion dome around the shelter corals so they arc smoothly above the branches.
  - **Consistent upward tangential steering & scaled escape routes:** Fixes tangential avoidance so odd-indexed fish are never pushed downward onto bottom corals, preserves outward repulsion during tangential passes, scales short escape routes to fish size and speed, and replaces blocked goals near the shelter-coral pockets promptly.
