# Ocean 1.9.0 — Battery-oriented rendering and expert energy options

Ocean now does less repeated work per frame:

- **Precomputed reef shadows (on by default):** the rigid reef's shadow is
  rendered once and reused. Only fish and swaying plants redraw their shadows
  each frame. The picture stays very close to 1.8.5; in places, especially with
  distance-softened shadows, small reef shadows can look slightly different, for
  example a closer shadow between overlapping coral plates. You can switch this
  off in the settings under **Experten / Energie** (Expert / Energy).
- Water background drawn after the scenery, light-ray parameters prepared once
  per frame, fewer wake-ups between frames, fewer engine worker threads, and
  closed coral bodies no longer draw their hidden back faces.
- Fish and study colour textures use ASTC compression where the graphics chip
  supports it, with the previous textures as fallback. The APK is about 7 MB larger.

**New expert dialog** (German UI: *Experten / Energie*): render resolution,
tilt sensor rate, 15 FPS while the notification shade or app overview covers
the wallpaper (only if the launcher reports it), optional idle reduction to 30
or 20 FPS after 30 seconds without interaction, ambient occlusion, compact colour
buffer, earlier fish detail switching and the precomputed reef shadows. Defaults
keep the established picture; your selected 30/40/50/60 FPS remains the upper limit.

On an Android emulator, controlled frozen-scene comparisons with the precomputed
shadow switched off matched 1.8.5 for 99.93–99.97% of pixels, with nearly all
others within one colour level. Native lifecycle, surface-recreation and
screen-off/resume checks passed, as did four system previews alongside an
active Ocean wallpaper. **Battery use, frame rate and temperature on phones have
not been measured**; emulator timings do not establish a saving.

The public update from 1.8.5 to 1.9.0 passed through Ocean and Android's installer
on the dedicated API-36 emulator. Lighting options, 40% caustic speed, 30 FPS,
shadow quality and fish/growth settings were retained. The installed APK matches
the public download; temporary installation permission was turned off again.
No crash, ANR, scan or block dialog was observed in this update test. This is
not a phone test or general Play Protect clearance.
