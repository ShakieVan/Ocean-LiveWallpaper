# Ocean 1.21.0 — Out of beta: everything since 1.13.0, and particles that ride the swell

Ocean 1.21.0 is the first release after the beta series. It brings together everything from 1.13.1 to 1.20.2 and adds one new detail:

- **New: suspended particles ride the swell.** With **Swell** on, the drifting particles now move with the same water as the fish: the same rhythm, direction and depth falloff. The wave reaches the particles at the back of the reef first. With Swell off, they move as before.

Collected from the beta series (compared with 1.13.0):

- **Camera and tilt:** spirit-level alignment can be switched off; the horizon stays level when turning around the vertical axis; a new **Camera mode** with the **On-site camera** that follows every phone rotation 1:1, and **Parallax**; a calm picture when the phone is switched on while still moving; the camera editor turns all the way round and almost straight up or down.
- **Water:** **Classic** or **Lively** surface waves; **Swell** and a **Water level** slider; sun rays that follow the waves and fade out smoothly toward the horizon.
- **Light:** light patterns on reef, sand, fish and plants modelled on a ray-traced reference, without flicker; with **Moving water light** off, the light is even and has no patterns.
- **Reef:** a reef all around the on-site camera, with sand to the horizon.
- **Fish:** smoother fins, and lighter young fish around the shelter corals.
- **Performance:** less processor and graphics work per frame with practically the same picture. The effect on battery life has not been measured.
- **Defaults:** new defaults for new installations; existing settings are kept when you update.

The individual beta release notes ([1.13.1](v1.13.1.md) to [1.20.2](v1.20.2.md)) have the details.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.21.0 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.21.0).
