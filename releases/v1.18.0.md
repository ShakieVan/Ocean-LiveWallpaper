# Ocean 1.18.0 (Beta) — Sun rays that follow the waves

Ocean 1.18.0 (Beta) makes the sun rays in the background water respond to the water surface above them:

- **Flat wave troughs** let the nearly parallel sunlight through: bright, clear rays below them.
- **Sharp crests and steep wave flanks** scatter the light: below them the rays fade, get wider or are bent aside.
- Rays now **appear, vanish, widen and narrow** in time with the waves instead of sweeping like searchlights. With lively waves this happens quickly; the classic surface stays calmer.
- With **swell** switched on, broader and brighter bands roll through the rays.
- The rays are now twice as dense and half as wide, and they **end at the water surface** — looking up, they no longer shine through it.

On a Galaxy S21 the graphics load stayed unchanged within measurement noise. The light patterns on the reef are unchanged.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.18.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.18.0).
