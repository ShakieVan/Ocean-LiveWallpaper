# Ocean 1.11.2 — Natural mid- and lower-reef swimming depth for large fish

Ocean 1.11.2 restores natural vertical swimming paths through the lower and middle coral canyon for large species (Butterflyfish, Regal Tang and Pennant Coralfish) while keeping zero stuck or bouncing contacts around the shelter corals (*Pocillopora*):

- **Natural vertical swimming distribution across the reef:**
  - **Removed global upward steering & height lifts:** Replaces the overly broad upward normal/tangent biases and foreground height lifts from 1.11.1 with horizontal tangential obstacle avoidance (`tangent.y = 0`) and exact spherical shelter-coral clearance, so large fish no longer drift toward the top edge of the screen.
  - **Open-water foreground goal fallback:** When a lower-screen goal candidate intersects the back reef wall, the goal generator tries placing it in the open foreground water at the same depth before resampling height, preserving lower- and mid-reef swimming paths.
  - **Targeted shelter-coral goal redirection:** If a large fish approaches a shelter coral while its current goal lies directly behind that coral, it smoothly selects a new clear goal in open water instead of pressing against or orbiting the branches.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.11.2 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.11.2).
