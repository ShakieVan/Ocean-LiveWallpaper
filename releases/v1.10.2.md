# Ocean 1.10.2 — Bright bulb-tipped anemones without blue sheen, Clownfish pairs, tentacle-parting cuddle courses and foreground camera passes

Ocean 1.10.2 redesigns the anemone colonies to match the reference morphology and colouration, doubles the Clownfish into resident pairs at both anemones, opens the swimming volume right through the middle of the anemone tentacles, and adds prominent foreground passes in front of the active camera:

- **Newly sculpted, creamy-white bulb-tipped anemones without bluish sheen:**
  - **Cylindrical stalks with rounded "snail-eyestalk" bulbous tips:** each tentacle is built around its curved 3D spine tangent with an 8-sided circular cross-section and 12 vertical rings whose upper 6 rings form a true spherical/ovoid terminal bulb above a slender neck rather than a 4-sided pointed cone.
  - **Bright creamy-white / pearl-white colouration:** tentacles transition from a soft blush-ivory base to bright milky/pearl white on the rounded terminal knobs, with the former turquoise basal saucers, blue tentacle bases and dark longitudinal stripes removed.
  - **No bluish world/water-light glare:** the anemone shader uses diffuse-only water caustics (`OCEAN_WATER_DIFFUSE_ONLY`), zero `clearCoat`, low dielectric `reflectance` (`0.10`) and soft `roughness` (`0.72`), eliminating the blue grazing specular sheen.
- **Clownfish pairs, unobstructed anemone cuddling & prominent camera passes:**
  - **Clownfish pairs at both anemones:** even at the default 16-fish count, two complete Clownfish pairs (a slightly larger female and slightly smaller male at both the right anemone `[4.35, -3.8, 0.25]` and the left anemone `[-0.35, -3.48, 3.05]`) inhabit the reef.
  - **Direct tentacle weaving & 4-swimmer tentacle deflection:** coarse overhanging rock colliders no longer push Clownfish to the outer edge of the right anemone; during anemone mode, both pairs weave back and forth right through the heart of the tentacles while up to 4 anemonefish simultaneously part the full length of nearby tentacles.
  - **Prominent foreground camera passes:** every fourth cycle, each Clownfish temporarily releases its anemone tether and glides up to `2.15–2.70 m` directly in front of the active camera view before returning to its anemone partner.

All existing settings, frame-rate choices (30, 40, 50 and 60 FPS), fish counts and coral layouts remain unchanged. **Phone battery savings, frame rate and temperature have not been measured.**
