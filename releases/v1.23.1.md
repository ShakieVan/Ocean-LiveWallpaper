# Ocean 1.23.1 (Beta) — The swell rolls over you

Ocean 1.23.1 (Beta) reworks how the swell shows on the water surface:

- **No more fizzling out:** the swell used to charge at the camera at the far rim of the surface, pull the surface down and then fade to a mild wave before the reef. It is a gentle, long wave on a flat surface sheet, so it showed mostly at flat view angles. It now grows on its way toward you, like a wave running up on a reef: unchanged in the distance, up to 2.5 times as high overhead. This is a deliberate exaggeration for the picture, not a physical simulation.
- **Surge:** the small waves are carried back and forth by the swell, the same water motion that moves the fish and the suspended particles. Straight overhead the surface now slides forward and back with each wave.
- **Rougher crests:** the small waves are a little steeper on the crests of the swell and flatter in its troughs.
- **Waves overhead:** looking up, the bright window in the surface showed one flat colour. It now shows the wave ridges with a light and a dark side, also without swell.

The swell setting, "Dünung" under "Licht & Wasser" (Light & Water) with off, gentle and strong, is unchanged; gentle halves everything, including the growth. Fish, particles, plants and the light on the reef move as before. Reef, fish, settings and updater are unchanged.

The extra work is a few calculations per pixel of the surface. Neither that cost nor battery use has been measured. The release was checked on a Galaxy S21 and on an emulator. This is a beta: the strength of the effect may still change.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.23.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.23.1).
