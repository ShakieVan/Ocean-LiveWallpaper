# Ocean 1.8.1 — Dancing caustics and wave-shaped reflections

**Moving water light** now creates finer, livelier light networks across the
seabed, reef, fish and small schools. Bright bands stretch, split and reconnect
as shorter waves overlap. Slower changes in overall illumination remain
as a gentler background effect.

Fish highlights now use a moving water-surface light pattern that changes
with the waves and viewing angle. The distance-softened shadows introduced
in 1.8.0 keep their existing behaviour.

Enable **Settings > Moving water light** (`Bewegtes Wasserlicht`). Existing
switch positions and other settings are preserved. Both water light and soft
shadows still default to off and can be enabled independently.

This remains an approximation without ray tracing. It adds graphics work;
phone frame-rate and battery impact have not been measured.

[Watch the native Android light preview](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/media/ocean-water-light-181.mp4).
This 3.2-second clip contains 48 native emulator test frames sampled at 15
time points per second. Fish pose and camera are held fixed so the light
motion is easy to see; waves and plant sway advance. It is not a device-FPS
measurement. The optional soft shadows are enabled.

All eight fish types, switch combinations, surface recreation, repeated
system previews alongside an active wallpaper, and screen resume passed
native Android checks.
