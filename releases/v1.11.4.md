# Ocean 1.11.4 — Sharper coral, rock and fish surface detail without extra GPU texture cost

Ocean 1.11.4 sharpens coral, reef rock and fish textures across default settings while keeping texture memory, texture sample counts and the **Rock colour** slider unchanged:

- **Synchronised, higher-frequency reef micro-detail:**
  - **Phase-locked pigment and micro-normals:** Removes the `0.52×` world-scale downscaling on coral pigment detail so the triplanar pore and growth-ridge projection uses the exact same world coordinates (`2.4–4.2` repeats per unit) as its paired micro-normal map, more than doubling linear pore and ridge frequency on plate, brain, sponge, gorgonian and fleshy corals while aligning dark pore centres with their normal-map craters.
  - **Crisper pore and crevice contrast:** Expands local cavity darkening (`1.55×` below mean luminance) and ridge highlights (`1.28×` above mean) around each surface family's linear mean without shifting overall brightness or fluorescence masks, and extends mid-distance retention on the default **High** surface-detail setting.
- **Full baked normal relief on reef rocks and plates:**
  - **Corrected UV derivative fade on photo-mapped rocks and corals:** Fixes the normal-map distance fade to evaluate `fwidth(getUV0())` instead of the `4×`-tiled `libraryUV` on photo-mapped rocks, preventing the 512×512 high-mesh normal map from fading out prematurely in the foreground and strengthening baked crevice ambient occlusion while keeping the **Rock colour** slider (natural limestone to grey reef stone to dark volcanic rock) fully intact.
- **4× anisotropic filtering and crisper fish scales:**
  - **Oblique-angle clarity:** Enables `4×` hardware anisotropic filtering on fish skin, fin and study-coral samplers so fish flanks and fins remain sharp during turns and oblique views.
  - **Resolved scale lips at default settings:** Extends mid-range resolution and adds subtle baseline scale-lip definition on the default **High** surface-detail setting with zero additional texture fetches.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.11.4 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.11.4).
