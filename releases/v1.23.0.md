# Ocean 1.23.0 — Wilder waves without the checkered pattern

Ocean 1.23.0 reworks the lively water surface:

- **No more checkered pattern:** looking left, right or diagonally, the lively waves showed a net of crossing straight lines. Each wave was an endless, perfectly straight wave train. Seen along its crests, such a train turns into ruled lines to the horizon, and the lines of the different trains crossed at acute angles. Every crest now bends along its length and slowly drifts sideways, and each wave has its own bend length, start and drift. The crests are short and irregular, as wind waves are.
- **Wave strength:** a new slider, "Wellenstärke (lebhaft)" under "Licht & Wasser" (Light & Water), makes the lively waves flatter or steeper, from 50 to 300 %. Higher values show more dark flanks and highlights and make the sun rays more restless. 100 % is the surface as it was.
- **Unrest:** a second new slider, "Unruhe (lebhaft)", lets the wave strength vary from place to place: rougher and calmer patches drift over the surface with the waves. It goes from off to 100 % and starts off.

Both sliders act on the **Lively** waves only and take effect at once. With their defaults, only the curved crests change the picture. Reef, fish and updater are unchanged.

The curved crests cost the graphics chip more. Measured on a Galaxy S21 looking straight up, with the surface filling the screen, a frame took 5 to 10 % more GPU time, depending on the number of waves; full unrest added about 2 %. Battery use has not been measured. The release was checked on that one phone model and on an emulator.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.23.0 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.23.0).
