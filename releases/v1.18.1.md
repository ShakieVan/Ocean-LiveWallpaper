# Ocean 1.18.1 (Beta) — Sun rays after a ray-traced reference

For this release the sun rays were first computed physically on a PC: nearly parallel sunlight through the app's own wave surfaces, scattered by clear tropical water. Ocean 1.18.1 (Beta) imitates that reference cheaply on the phone:

- Rays are **brightest just below the surface and get steadily weaker toward the depth**, instead of peaking in the middle of the picture.
- They are **wider** and **drift sideways with the waves**, while still appearing and vanishing as before.
- **Looking up**, the rays now lie in front of the water surface, and they no longer converge into a starburst around the sun.
- Brightness of the strongest rays matches 1.18.0.

On a Galaxy S21 the graphics load stayed unchanged within measurement noise looking forward, and about 1 % higher looking up.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.18.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.18.1).
