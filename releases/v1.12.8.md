# Ocean 1.12.8 (Beta) — Co-rotating X/Y tilt axes with 3rd-axis Z-rotation

Ocean 1.12.8 (Beta) rotates the X/Y camera tilt axes (`alignedX`, `alignedY`) in `ParallaxCamera.update` by the active 3rd-axis Z-rotation angle (`roll`):

- **Co-rotating X/Y tilt axes (`ParallaxCamera.update`):**
  - When the phone is rotated around Z (for example, $90^\circ$ into Landscape while system home/lock-screen rotation is locked to Portrait), pitching the phone forward or backward rotates around the phone's physical Y-axis (`inputX`).
  - `ParallaxCamera.update` now projects the phone-frame tilt vector `(-inputX, inputY)` onto the roll-stabilized ocean horizon axes using the same `cos(roll)` and `sin(roll)` values already computed for `updateUpVector` (zero extra trigonometric calls per frame).
  - Tilting the phone forward/backward now always pitches the ocean camera vertically (up/down) and tilting left/right always pans horizontally (left/right) at any Z-rotation angle ($0^\circ$, $\pm 90^\circ$ Landscape, $180^\circ$, or any intermediate angle).

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.12.8 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.12.8).
