# Ocean 1.2.0 — More coral variety, giant clams and denser growth

Six new coral forms double the reef's visual variety from six to twelve:
rounded fingers, branching antlers, curled scrolls, deep cups, bubble clusters
and slender organ pipes with star-shaped polyps. These are visual growth types,
not a claim to twelve identified biological species.

- **Choose each type:** two menus replace the coral-variety slider:
  **Fish species** (`Fischarten`) and **Corals & clams** (`Korallen & Muscheln`).
  Turn each of the eight fish types and thirteen reef types on or off, or use
  **All on / All off**. Tap an eye icon for a small portrait of that model;
  all 21 portraits are rendered from Ocean's actual 3D models.
- **Fish count:** the selected count is distributed among the enabled fish
  species. With every fish species off, no fish are shown; the saved count is
  retained for when you turn a species back on.
- **Giant clams:** three additional animals, separate from the coral count.
  Each has a ridged shell and a bright, striped mantle that pulses subtly
  inside the opening. Their original geometry uses mapped surface detail.
- **Extra growth:** twice as many additional colonies at every enabled step.
  With all coral types enabled, the existing 0–100% slider adds 0, 16, 32, 48
  or 62 colonies. It also follows your coral selections: a disabled type is
  removed from the additional growth too. Previous reef placements are preserved.
- **Mapped surfaces:** the new original shapes use the existing reef texture
  maps. Texture sharpness and selected fluorescent colours also work on these
  corals; lower brightness brings out the fluorescence.
- **Underwater light:** the moving rays extend farther to both sides of the view.

All types are enabled by default. An existing saved coral-variety selection is
converted to the matching individual choices. Your settings are saved for the
preview and live wallpaper. More coral types and growth add graphics work;
disable types or lower extra growth to draw fewer additional reef objects.
Actual performance and battery use depend on your phone. Existing camera,
appearance, fish and tilt settings remain available, including exactly
30, 40, 50 or 60 FPS.

Install **Ocean-1.2.0-universal.apk**, or open **Settings > Updates** in Ocean.
Android supplies the wallpaper destination picker, so available home/lock-screen
combinations depend on your phone. The app interface is currently German;
see the [control guide](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#coral-variety-and-extra-growth).
