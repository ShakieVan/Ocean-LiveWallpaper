# Ocean 1.23.2 (Beta) — The same picture with less work

Ocean 1.23.2 (Beta) removes graphics work that repeated itself or was never shown. The picture stays the same:

- **Light on reef, sand and plants:** a few values were calculated twice for every pixel. They are now calculated once.
- **Soft reef shadows:** sunlit surfaces skip a step that only shadowed surfaces need.
- **Light shafts:** adding up the shafts takes fewer calculations per pixel.
- **Fish:** the gentle breathing of the gill covers is only calculated near the gills.
- **Start and memory:** two textures from an early version were still loaded at every start, although nothing draws them any more. Ocean now starts without them, which frees about 17 MB of graphics memory.

To check that nothing visible changed, frozen views of the old and the new version were compared pixel by pixel. On a Galaxy S21, 19 of 83 million pixels differ, by at most 2 of 255 brightness levels. On an emulator, 308 of 176 million pixels differ, by at most 3 levels.

The savings are small and have not been measured; no battery or frame-rate claim is made. Reef, fish, settings and updater are unchanged. This is a beta.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.23.2 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.23.2).
