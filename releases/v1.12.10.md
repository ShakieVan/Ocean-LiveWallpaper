# Ocean 1.12.10 (Beta) — Docked Liquid-Glass menu handle, bottom-center wallpaper icon, and background update downloads

Ocean 1.12.10 (Beta) refines the main preview screen, the Liquid-Glass settings wheel, and the in-app updater:

- **Docked Liquid-Glass menu with central left-edge handle ("Wulst"):**
  - The left border of the settings menu now features an integrated central Liquid-Glass bulge housing the settings icon at the vertical center of the right screen edge.
  - When collapsed, the menu sits pushed out to the right so that ~1 mm of its left glass rim remains visible along the right border while the central icon handle protrudes into the screen.
  - Pull the handle to the left or tap it to open the menu; tap anywhere outside the menu, swipe right, or tap the handle again to collapse it back to the edge.
- **Bottom-center Liquid-Glass "Set wallpaper" icon & cleaner main view:**
  - Replaced the bottom bar with a floating Liquid-Glass wallpaper icon button centered at the bottom of the screen, and removed the duplicate main-screen coral shuffle button (available live inside **Reef & Growth > Redistribute corals**).
- **Continuous background update downloads & instant install offer on app switch:**
  - Update downloads now continue running in the background when switching to other apps.
  - Whenever an update APK has already finished downloading (including while using another app), returning to Ocean immediately offers **Install update** directly in the settings menu and update dialog.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.12.10 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.12.10).
