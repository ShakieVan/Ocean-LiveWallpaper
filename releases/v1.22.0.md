# Ocean 1.22.0 — Fish that swim through water

Ocean 1.22.0 reworks how the fish move:

- **No more twitching:** the fish no longer jerk when they dodge each other, set off, give chase or settle on their anemone rock. Bend, bank and fin strength now ease toward their target the way a body does in water.
- **Strokes that fit the fish:** every species covers a fixed part of its body length per tail beat. Small fish beat fast, large ones slowly, and all of them faster when they speed up.
- **Fins first, body after:** when a fish sets off, the tail works at once and the body follows a moment later. When it coasts, the tail rests first and the body glides on. At cruising speed the fish row with their pectoral fins and bring in the tail to accelerate and turn; to slow down they spread the pectorals.
- **Fins trail their base:** a fin is moved at its base only, and the water holds the rest back. The tail blade now bends against the stroke like a diver's fin, slightly when the fish fans slowly and strongly on a hard stroke. The dorsal and anal fins, rigid until now, trail the body's sideways motion.
- **Braking at the reef:** a fish heading for the reef slows while it turns away instead of running on and stopping dead.

The existing slider for flow and water mechanics ("Strömung & Wassermechanik" under "Fische & Korallen") still scales all of this; at 0 % the fish move as they did in 1.21.2. No setting was added or changed. Frame rate and battery use on a phone have not been measured for this release.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.22.0 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.22.0).
