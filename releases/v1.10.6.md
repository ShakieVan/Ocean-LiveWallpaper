# Ocean 1.10.6

- **Right anemone colony positioning & wider camera rotation:** Shifts the right 10-crown anemone colony and its 2-slab rock foundation half an anemone width to the left (`[3.30, -3.96, 2.50]`) for better framing, and expands horizontal camera rotation (`cameraYaw`) by $\pm 30^\circ$ from $\pm 25^\circ$ to $\pm 55^\circ$.
- **Shadow-pipeline & shader power optimisations:** Uses hardware `DEPTH16` `PCF` for dynamic moving-fish/plant cascades whenever precomputed 2048×2048 soft EVSSM reef shadows (`StaticReefShadow`) are active (eliminating 21 per-frame RGBA16F Gaussian mipmap passes and a duplicate per-pixel EVSSM evaluation while keeping soft precomputed reef shadows intact), uses a single hardware bilinear tap for contact shadows within 1.25 texels in `static-shadow.glsl`, skips secondary/tertiary triplanar `textureGrad` fetches on non-diagonal reef surfaces (`weights` cutoff `0.10`), skips `animatedMapFrame()` on flat/distant reef normals, removes an unused `textureLod` face lookup and gates 4-tap detail/gill/fluorescence work on fish shaders, compiles only active `shadowReceiver` material states in the background, uses hardware bilinear scaling when `renderScalePercent < 100`, and removes `FLAG_DIM_BEHIND` on the settings sheet.

Phone battery savings have not been measured.
