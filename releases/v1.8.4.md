# Ocean 1.8.4 — More natural reef lighting

Moving water light no longer adds a blue reflective rim to rocks and coral
bases. The moving caustic patterns still illuminate the reef, while fish retain
their wave-shaped reflections.

Existing settings, fluorescence and distance-softened shadows are preserved.
Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the
[1.8.4 release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.8.4).

Native Android emulator checks passed for the lighting switches, caustic-speed
control, surface recreation and app lifecycle. All 24 isolated fish comparison
images match the previous version pixel for pixel. The signed release also
passed four system previews alongside an active Ocean wallpaper and a
screen-off/resume check. This is not a phone performance or battery measurement.

The public update from 1.8.3 to 1.8.4 passed through Ocean and Android's installer
on a dedicated API-36 emulator. Enabled lighting options, 40% caustic speed,
30 FPS and fish/growth settings were retained. The installed APK matches the
public download, and temporary installation permission was switched off again.
No crash, ANR, scan or block dialog was observed during this update test.
This does not establish general Play Protect clearance.

Version **1.8.4 / code 32**. Android's system wallpaper picker continues to
determine the available home-screen and lock-screen destinations.
