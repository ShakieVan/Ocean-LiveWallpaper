# Ocean 1.26.0 (Beta) — A grounded reef

Ocean 1.26.0 (Beta) puts the reef on solid ground and gives the giant clam its shell:

- **Rocks rest on the seabed:** the rocks of the middle and back reef no longer hover above the sand; they sit in it like the front rocks. Stacked rocks rest on the rocks below them, the bridge lies on its pillars, and stacked rocks that only touched with an edge now stand on foundation rocks hidden behind and below them. The fish tunnel under the bridge stays open.
- **Corals sit on rock:** in all four coral distributions, colonies that hung over rock edges, hung upside down under overhangs or sat too deep in the rock now grow on supporting rock, among them a brain coral that floated beside a rock and a giant clam that sat half inside one. Your chosen distribution stays; some of its colonies are now in new places.
- **A real shell for the giant clam:** two large, light, ribbed valves with an interlocking zig-zag rim. The blue mantle lies in the gap between them.
- **Big fish keep clear of the nurseries:** large fish no longer brush along the shelter corals of the clownfish nurseries.
- **No fish left behind:** after the on-site camera view, no fish stays stuck behind the camera.
- **Cleaner precomputed shadows:** with "Vorberechnete Riffschatten" (precomputed reef shadows) and soft shadows on, the thin dark lines at the foot of rocks are gone, and broad soft shadows no longer fade.

Settings, updater and signing key are unchanged.

## How to update

- **From 1.24.1, 1.24.2 or 1.25.0:** through **Settings > Updates**.
- **From 1.24.0 or older:** their updater offers **1.24.1** first, which stays marked "Latest" on GitHub for this reason. After that update, open Ocean once more and 1.26.0 is offered. Alternatively, download the APK below and install it directly over your current version: Android keeps your settings.

## Known limits

- Checked on an API-36 Android emulator, not on phones. Battery use was not measured.
- The lock screen itself was not looked at. This is a beta.
