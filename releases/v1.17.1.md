# Ocean 1.17.1 (Beta) — A reef ring that stands on solid ground

Ocean 1.17.1 (Beta) reworks the rocks around the **On-site camera** after feedback on 1.17.0:

- Rocks are now firmly embedded in the sand; nothing floats above the ground any more.
- Stacked rocks get narrower toward the top and sit firmly on each other — no more top-heavy towers or wide overhanging slabs.
- All ring rocks stay well below the water surface, even at the lowest **Water level**.
- The forward view is unchanged; looking backwards is cheaper than in 1.17.0, and the app download is smaller. No general battery or FPS claim is made for other phones.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.17.1 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.17.1).
