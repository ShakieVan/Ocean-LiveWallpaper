# Ocean 1.12.0 — 5× adjustable & invertible gyroscope parallax, sharper coral/rock/fish surface detail, and natural shelter-coral fish routing

Ocean 1.12.0 consolidates all improvements since 1.11.0 into a major update featuring responsive gyroscope depth tracking with up to 5× wider **Tilt limits** and an **Invert tilt direction** switch, crisp normal-distance coral pores, rock relief, foreground *Pocillopora* shelter corals and fish scales at zero extra GPU texture cost, and natural mid/lower-reef swimming routes around the shelter corals:

- **Responsive gyroscope parallax, 5× tilt limits & invertible tilt direction:**
  - **Adjustable Tilt limits (`100%–500%`, up to 5× range):** A new **Tilt limits** (*Neigungsgrenzen*) slider directly below **Tilt strength** keeps the same calm sensitivity per degree of tilt near center while opening the gyroscope clamp from the default ~18° wrist angle (`±3.9°` horizontal / `±3.0°` vertical orbit) up to **5× wider** (`±19.6°` horizontal / `±15.1°` vertical spherical orbit around the chosen pivot) as you continue tilting the device up to ~90°, with gimbal-free vertical angle extraction ($\operatorname{atan2}(r_7, \sqrt{r_6^2 + r_8^2})$).
  - **Invert tilt direction switch (`invertTilt`, default off):** Reverses both horizontal and vertical gyroscope camera orbit directions smoothly while preserving your chosen **Tilt strength**, **Tilt limits** and **Pivot distance**.
  - **Immediate parallax depth tracking & resilient sensor fallback:** Matches camera low-pass filter rates to the selected **Expert / Energy > Tilt response** stage (`28.0 1/s` / ~36 ms time constant on *fast* instead of a fixed 200 ms filter, `14.0 1/s` / ~71 ms on *normal*, and `5.0 1/s` / 200 ms on *power-saving*), increases natural wrist-angle sensitivity (`GAIN = 3.2`), declares `HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS`, and automatically falls back across `SENSOR_DELAY_GAME → SENSOR_DELAY_UI → SENSOR_DELAY_NORMAL` and rotation-vector sensors if a delivery rate is rejected.
- **Sharper coral pores, rock crevices, foreground shelter corals & fish scales at zero extra GPU cost:**
  - **Phase-locked triplanar pores & normal-distance visibility:** Synchronises world-space coordinates (`scale = profile.y` at `2.4–4.2` repeats per world unit) between triplanar pore pigment and micro-normals, widens world-footprint fade thresholds (`smoothstep(42, 110, footprint)` and `smoothstep(18, 48, footprint)`), and biases `textureGrad` mip derivatives (`0.55×`) so fine pores and craters stay in sharp `Mip 0/1` resolution at normal portrait camera distance (`Camera zoom: 100%`).
  - **Direct slider coupling & full rock relief with Rock colour support:** Connects **Texture sharpness** and **Detail contours** directly to triplanar pore/ridge micro-contrast and 16-ray crevice ambient occlusion (`surface.a`), fixes `fwidth(getUV0())` normal-map distance fading on photo rocks and procedural corals, and preserves the **Rock colour** slider (`reefTone`) across limestone (`0%`), dark grey (`50%`) and magma black (`100%`).
  - **Crisp foreground *Pocillopora* shelter corals:** Restores normal-map relief (`0.15 → 0.58`), polyp contrast, crevice AO and **Texture sharpness / Detail contours** response (`10% → 78%`) on the large foreground branching shelter corals while retaining their wet mucus sheen (`clearCoat = 0.88`).
  - **4× anisotropic fish filtering & mid-canyon scale contours:** Enables `4×` anisotropic filtering on all fish samplers, extends scale resolution into mid-canyon swimming distances, and strengthens scale-pocket contrast (`±28%`) and scale-lip outlines (`scaleLine`) with zero additional texture fetches or memory (`116.326 MiB` per engine unchanged).
- **Size-aware shelter-coral avoidance & natural mid/lower-reef swimming depth for large fish:**
  - **Isotropic spherical shelter-coral & pedestal colliders:** Replaces flattened ellipsoid colliders around the shelter corals (`r = 0.86`) and anemone rock foundations with isotropic spheres, adds larger clearance margins (`aheadPad = 0.38`, `nowPad = 0.28`) for large fish (Butterflyfish, Regal Tang and Pennant Coralfish), and redirects goals set directly behind a shelter coral (`redirectAwayFromShelterCoral`) so large fish never get stuck or bounce on the branches.
  - **Natural vertical distribution across the lower and mid canyon (73–91% residency):** Uses purely horizontal tangential obstacle steering (`tangent.y = 0`) and open-foreground goal fallback for lower/mid-reef targets so large fish swim naturally throughout the lower and middle reef instead of drifting toward the top edge of the screen.
