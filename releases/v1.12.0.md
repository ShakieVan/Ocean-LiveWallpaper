# Ocean 1.12.0 — Docked Liquid-Glass register selection wheel, 3rd gyroscope axis with spirit-level horizon, 5× tilt limits, background update downloads, sharper coral/rock/fish detail and natural shelter-coral fish routing

Ocean 1.12.0 consolidates all improvements since 1.11.0 into a major update featuring a right-edge **Liquid-Glass register selection wheel** (*Auswahlrad*) and bottom-center **Set wallpaper** icon button, a dedicated **Tilt & Gyroscope** submenu with the **3rd gyroscope axis ("Rotate screen")** and **5× adjustable Tilt limits**, background update downloads across app switches, crisp normal-distance coral pores, rock relief, foreground *Pocillopora* shelter corals and `4×` anisotropic fish scales at zero extra GPU cost, and natural mid/lower-reef swimming routes around the shelter corals:

- **Right-edge Liquid-Glass register selection wheel & bottom-center wallpaper icon button:**
  - **Docked at the right screen edge with a central left-edge icon bulge (`R.id.settings_open`):** When collapsed, only ~1 mm of the left menu border peeks into the screen on the right edge while the central Liquid-Glass bulge with the settings icon protrudes cleanly into the view. Open the wheel by **pulling left** (with live finger tracking) or **tapping** the bulge; collapse it again by **tapping outside the menu**, swiping right, or pulling/tapping the bulge.
  - **Register wheel, custom vector pictograms & horizontal submenu transitions:** Entries scroll vertically through a fixed central selection zone, smoothly unfolding like register cards (`RegisterCardView`) with custom vector pictograms (`PictogramDrawable`) while curving, scaling and fading toward the top and bottom edges so the live 3D reef stays visible while adjusting settings. Submenus (*Tilt & Gyroscope*, *Fish & Motion*, *Reef & Growth*, *Light & Water*, *Surface & Colours*, *Energy & Expert*) slide in horizontally from the right and return via `< Back` or a right-swipe.
  - **Bottom-center Liquid-Glass "Set wallpaper" button:** Replaces the dark bottom action bar with a circular Liquid-Glass icon button (`LiquidGlassWallpaperButton`) at the bottom center of the screen (coral redistribution remains directly accessible with live preview under **Reef & Growth > Redistribute corals**).
- **3rd gyroscope axis ("Rotate screen"), 5× tilt limits, invertible directions & "Tilt & Gyroscope" submenu:**
  - **Dedicated Tilt & Gyroscope submenu:** Consolidates **Tilt strength**, **Tilt limits** (`100%–500%`), **Pivot distance** (`1–5`), **Invert tilt direction**, **Rotate screen (3rd axis)**, **Invert rotation direction**, and **Tilt response** (*power-saving* / *normal* / *fast*).
  - **3rd gyroscope axis (`tiltRoll`, default off) & separate Invert rotation direction (`invertTiltRoll`, default off):**
    - Uses the true 1:1 rotation angle ($\operatorname{atan2}$) around the camera view axis (`upX`, `upY`, `upZ`) across full $360^\circ$ rotations (never flipping at $180^\circ$), synchronized with touch projection for the shelter-coral fish schools (`NurserySchools.project`).
    - **1:1 spirit-level (*Wasserwaage*) horizon & flat-screen exception:** Aligns the ocean horizon 1:1 with gravity in upright and tilted postures (`planar > FLAT_THRESHOLD`), while aligning with the phone screen (`0 rad`) when the device lies nearly flat ($\sqrt{g_x^2 + g_y^2} \le 0.16$).
    - **Drift-free 3D sensor math even during simultaneous tilting and rotation (`advanceRoll`):** Combines allocation-free incremental column dot products (`decoupledRollStep`) and a flat-posture `[0.60, 0.95]` smoothstep gate on `dz * weight * (1f - tiltBlend)` with the un-gated, holonomy-free gravity-meridian differential `dGrav * tiltBlend` in non-flat postures, keeping X/Y tilting, back-and-forth Z-rotation, and simultaneous X/Y tilting + Z-rotation completely free of horizon skew or drift.
    - **Horizon-rotated X/Y tilt axes (`ParallaxCamera.update`):** Rotates the phone's X/Y tilt vector `(-inputX, inputY)` by the active horizon roll angle (reusing `cos(roll)` and `sin(roll)` from `updateUpVector` with zero extra trig calls), so pitching a landscape-held or upside-down phone forward/backward always tilts the ocean camera vertically (up/down) relative to the horizon.
    - **Lock-screen rotation blockade, 2-second post-unlock full reset & 5-second quiet re-alignment:** Blocks 3rd-axis rotation (`roll = 0 rad`) while the lock screen is active (`KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked`), waits 2 seconds after unlocking (`POST_UNLOCK_DELAY_NANOS`, synchronized across lock/home engine handoffs), and then executes a smooth ($\tau \approx 0.17\text{ s}$) full reset of X/Y tilt (`baseline`) and 1:1 spirit-level horizon (`horizonRoll`), plus gentle automatic re-alignment ($\tau \approx 0.4\text{ s}$) after **5 seconds of quiet holding** (`isQuiet`, ignoring tremors within $5^\circ$ and brief jolts under $250\text{ ms}$).
  - **Adjustable Tilt limits (`100%–500%`), Invert tilt direction & responsive depth tracking:**
    - **Tilt limits** (`tiltLimitPercent`) keeps the same calm sensitivity per degree near center (`GAIN = 3.2`) while opening the gyroscope clamp from the default ~18° wrist angle (`±3.9°` horizontal / `±3.0°` vertical orbit) up to **5× wider** (`±19.6°` horizontal / `±15.1°` vertical spherical orbit around the chosen pivot) with gimbal-free vertical angle extraction ($\operatorname{atan2}(r_7, \sqrt{r_6^2 + r_8^2})$).
    - Preserves the original default tilt direction (`-inputX` horizontal, `+inputY` vertical when **Invert tilt direction** is off) and adds the **Invert tilt direction** switch (`invertTilt`).
    - Matches camera low-pass filter rates to **Tilt response** (`28.0 1/s` / ~36 ms on *fast*, `14.0 1/s` / ~71 ms on *normal*, and `5.0 1/s` / 200 ms on *power-saving*) with `HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS` and automatic fallback across `SENSOR_DELAY_GAME → SENSOR_DELAY_UI → SENSOR_DELAY_NORMAL`.
- **Background update downloads & immediate install offer:**
  - Ongoing update downloads continue running in the background when switching to another app (`MainActivity.onStop()`) or closing the dialog, automatically verifying the APK signature and SHA-256 checksum and immediately offering **Install update** (`Bereit · <Version>`) in the settings wheel upon returning to Ocean.
- **Sharper coral pores, rock crevices, foreground shelter corals & fish scales at zero extra GPU cost:**
  - **Phase-locked triplanar pores & normal-distance visibility:** Synchronises world-space coordinates (`scale = profile.y` at `2.4–4.2` repeats per world unit) between triplanar pore pigment and micro-normals, widens world-footprint fade thresholds (`smoothstep(42, 110, footprint)` and `smoothstep(18, 48, footprint)`), and biases `textureGrad` mip derivatives (`0.55×`) so fine pores and craters stay in sharp `Mip 0/1` resolution at normal portrait camera distance (`Camera zoom: 100%`).
  - **Direct slider coupling & full rock relief with Rock colour support:** Connects **Texture sharpness** and **Detail contours** directly to triplanar pore/ridge micro-contrast and 16-ray crevice ambient occlusion (`surface.a`), fixes `fwidth(getUV0())` normal-map distance fading on photo rocks and procedural corals, and preserves the **Rock colour** slider (`reefTone`) across limestone (`0%`), dark grey (`50%`) and magma black (`100%`).
  - **Crisp foreground *Pocillopora* shelter corals:** Restores normal-map relief (`0.15 → 0.58`), polyp contrast, crevice AO and **Texture sharpness / Detail contours** response (`10% → 78%`) on the large foreground branching shelter corals while retaining their wet mucus sheen (`clearCoat = 0.88`).
  - **4× anisotropic fish filtering & mid-canyon scale contours:** Enables `4×` anisotropic filtering on all fish samplers, extends scale resolution into mid-canyon swimming distances, and strengthens scale-pocket contrast (`±28%`) and scale-lip outlines (`scaleLine`) with zero additional texture fetches or memory (`116.326 MiB` per engine unchanged).
- **Size-aware shelter-coral avoidance & natural mid/lower-reef swimming depth for large fish:**
  - **Isotropic spherical shelter-coral & pedestal colliders:** Replaces flattened ellipsoid colliders around the shelter corals (`r = 0.86`) and anemone rock foundations with isotropic spheres, adds larger clearance margins (`aheadPad = 0.38`, `nowPad = 0.28`) for large fish (Butterflyfish, Regal Tang and Pennant Coralfish), and redirects goals set directly behind a shelter coral (`redirectAwayFromShelterCoral`) so large fish never get stuck or bounce on the branches.
  - **Natural vertical distribution across the lower and mid canyon (73–91% residency):** Uses purely horizontal tangential obstacle steering (`tangent.y = 0`) and open-foreground goal fallback for lower/mid-reef targets so large fish swim naturally throughout the lower and middle reef instead of drifting toward the top edge of the screen.
