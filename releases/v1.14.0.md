# Ocean 1.14.0 (Beta) — On-site camera: the reef camera follows every phone rotation 1:1

Ocean 1.14.0 (Beta) rebuilds the tilt camera on one whole 3D rotation and adds a **Camera mode** (*Kameraführung*) choice under **Tilt & Gyroscope**:

- **On-site camera (*Vor-Ort-Kamera*):** the camera in the reef repeats every rotation of the phone 1:1 in its own axes and turns about itself, without limits. Tilt the top of the phone back and it looks down; turn the phone and it pans; rotate the screen and it rolls. Combined and very fast motions are transferred exactly. A tilt strength of 100 % is exactly 1:1; lower values scale every rotation down.
- **Reference posture:** the posture at the last alignment (start, 5 seconds of holding still, unlocking) is the reference for the saved framing, whether the phone was upright, tilted in the lap or lying flat. The view then eases back to the saved framing without a jump. With **Spirit-level alignment** on, the reef horizon starts aligned with the real horizon.
- **Parallax (*Parallaxe*, default):** uses the same rotation, mirrored and limited, as an orbit around the pivot. The former posture-dependent roll heuristics are gone.
- **Rotate screen (3rd axis)** now rolls the camera in the same sense as the phone. If you had enabled **Invert rotation direction** to compensate, switch it off again. Without the 3rd axis, the on-site camera still follows the view direction 1:1 but keeps the horizon level.
- **Known limitation:** the reef is designed for looking forward. Looking sideways or backwards with the on-site camera shows open sand and water, a hard sand edge behind the camera, and the sand looks blocky when looking straight down.

Update through **Settings > Updates**, or download the universal APK from the [1.14.0 Beta release](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Ocean-LiveWallpaper/releases/tag/v1.14.0).
