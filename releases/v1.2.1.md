# Ocean 1.2.1 — Blue Maxima clams and adjustable fluorescence

The giant clam now has a deeply folded opening, a thick pale shell lip and a
dark mantle with a cobalt-blue longitudinal band. That blue band fluoresces;
the shell and dark mantle remain unlit. The model and markings are original.

- **Fluorescence slider:** adjust the glow independently of brightness in
  **Settings > Fluorescence** (`Einstellungen > Fluoreszenz`). Choose **0% to
  turn it off, 100% for normal strength, or up to 200% for an exaggerated effect**.
  The default is 100%, and the setting is saved for the preview and wallpaper.
- **Fish markings:** selected body lines, spots and tail accents now fluoresce
  on the existing fish and small nursery schools. They follow the moving fish
  and their mapped colours. These are artistic markings, not a claim about
  scientifically documented fluorescence in each depicted species.
- **Dark scenes:** lowering brightness dims the surroundings while compensation
  helps fluorescent markings remain visible. Brightness and glow strength can
  be adjusted separately.
- **Updated clam portrait:** the eye icon in **Corals & clams** shows a freshly
  rendered portrait of the new model. The individual species selections,
  doubled extra growth and extended side rays introduced in 1.2.0 remain available.
- **Close camera views:** ordinary swimming destinations leave more room for
  natural turns when zoomed in, helping fish remain visible without moving them
  instantly or changing their speed.

Install **Ocean-1.2.1-universal.apk**, or use **Settings > Updates** in Ocean.
The app interface is currently German. Android supplies the wallpaper destination
picker; available home/lock-screen combinations depend on your phone.

The new effects reuse existing textures and render passes. Actual performance
and battery use depend on your phone and settings; no phone battery measurement
is claimed for this release.
