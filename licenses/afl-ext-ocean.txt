Ocean modifications (2026-10-27): only the octave sum of `wavedx`/`getwaves`
is used, for an underside normal on a flat water ceiling. The slope is summed
analytically in the same loop instead of three height samples; no raymarching,
sky, atmosphere or tonemapping of the original is included. Up to 20 octaves
with precomputed directions, per-octave speeds rounded for a seamless time wrap,
footprint-based fading of sub-pixel octaves and a project-specific slope scale.
